# The week, seed 2

Elena, 42, the mother. Daniel, 25. Leo, 21. Mara, 17. The house is shut; the taps are dry; a can is missing from the shelf.

## Day 1

16 portions on the shelf. Elena and Leo woke warm. Mara woke on edge.

**Morning.** Elena asked who took the can: “There is a can missing. Which of you took it?”

Mara said it was not her: “I didn't take it, I swear.”

Daniel said it was not him: “I said no. I didn't take it.”

Leo said it was not him: “No. I didn't touch it.”

Elena set out the meal: “Come to the table. A share each.” Everyone ate.

Elena said the water would go out by the cup from now on: “The water goes out by the cup from now on. I will pour.”

Elena searched the kitchen for the can.

Daniel went out the back into the rain and set the bin under the drainpipe.

**The day's work.** Mara saw to the water; Elena counted the stores; Leo kept watch; Daniel went round the boards.

**Afternoon.** Leo: “Nothing moved in the street.” Elena: “14 portions on the shelf. I counted twice.”

**Evening.** Elena set out the meal: “Sit. Eat. Then we talk.” Everyone ate.

**Night.** Daniel, Elena and Mara lay awake; Leo slept.

## Day 2

12 portions on the shelf. Daniel woke on edge: a bad night. Elena woke on edge: a bad night. Mara woke on edge: a bad night.

**Morning.** Elena set out the meal: “Sit down, all of you. We eat first.” Everyone ate.

Mara: “I lay awake half the night.” Daniel: “I didn't sleep.”

**The day's work.** Mara saw to the water; Elena looked after Mara; Leo counted the stores; Daniel went round the boards. Mara strained her back carrying the water. Daniel cut his hand on the boards.

Mara to Elena: “Mom. Thank you. Really.” Elena to Mara: “It is nothing, Mara.” Daniel to Mara: “Mara, are you up to anything today?” Leo to Daniel: “Daniel, did you sleep at all?” Daniel to Leo: “Leave it, Leo. I'm up.” Mara to Daniel: “Don't fuss, Daniel.”

**Afternoon.** Leo: “I counted twice: 10 portions left.”

**Evening.** Elena set out a small meal: “I am cutting the shares. 10 portions, and nothing coming.” Everyone ate.

Mara: “I'm so hungry. Is that all there is?”

**Night.** Daniel and Leo slept; Elena and Mara lay awake.

## Day 3

9 portions on the shelf. Daniel woke low: a cut hand. Elena woke on edge: a bad night. Mara woke low: a strained back. Leo woke warm.

**Morning.** Leo to Mara: “How is it today, Mara?” Mara to Leo: “It hurts, Leo. I'm fine.”

Elena set out a small meal: “This is what there is today. Sit down.” Everyone ate.

Mara to Daniel: “Are you all right, Daniel? Really?” Daniel to Mara: “It's nothing, Mara. I can work.”

Daniel took Elena aside.

Daniel owned up, to Elena alone: “It was me. I took it. I ate it in the night.” *(what is on him)*

Elena had it out with Daniel: “You let me ask all of them, Daniel. You stood there.”

Daniel to Elena: “I have no excuse, Mom.”

**The day's work.** Daniel looked after Mara; Leo looked after Daniel; Elena saw to the water.

Mara to Daniel: “Thanks, Daniel. I mean it.”

Elena told the others who took the can: “Daniel took the can. He came and told me.”

Leo had it out with Daniel: “You said it was not you, Daniel. I heard you.”

Daniel to Leo: “I did not know how to say it, Leo.”

**Afternoon.** Daniel to Leo: “Leo. I owe you for that.”

**Evening.** Daniel set out a small meal: “Half each. I'll put it out.” *(making up for the can)* Everyone ate.

Daniel said he would go next door tonight: “I took from that shelf. I'll go next door tonight and put something back.”

Leo said nobody should go out: “No one goes out in the dark. Not yet.”

Elena told Daniel to stay home: “Daniel, you are not going out there. Not tonight.”

**Night.** Daniel and Mara lay awake; Elena sat up by the front door; Leo slept. Daniel was well again. Mara was well again.

## Day 4

7 portions on the shelf. Daniel woke on edge: a bad night and hunger and being told off yesterday. Elena woke on edge: a night on watch and yesterday's quarrel. Leo woke on edge: yesterday's quarrel. Mara woke on edge: a bad night.

**Morning.** Elena set out a small meal: “Small shares again. It has to last.” Everyone ate.

Mara had it out with Daniel: “I swore it wasn't me, Daniel. And you just stood there!”

Daniel to Mara: “I did not know how to say it, Mara.”

Elena lay down and slept.

**The day's work.** Elena saw to the water; Mara kept watch; Daniel went round the boards; Leo counted the stores.

**Afternoon.** Mara: “The street's empty. Nothing moved.” Leo: “I counted: 6 portions on the shelf.”

**Evening.** Elena set out a small meal: “This is what there is today. Sit down.” Everyone ate.

**Night.** Elena lay awake; Leo and Mara slept.

Daniel waited until the house was quiet and went out to the neighbour's house. *(meant to go)*

Elena heard the front door, got up, and waited by it until morning.

Daniel was back with water.

Daniel no longer meant to go next door: next door is empty.

## Day 5

5 portions on the shelf. Daniel woke low: a night out. Elena woke on edge: a night waiting up. Leo woke low: hunger. Mara woke on edge: hunger and yesterday's quarrel.

**Morning.** Elena set out a small meal: “Half shares. Eat slowly.” The others ate.

Elena gave her share to Daniel: “Eat mine, Daniel. I am not hungry.” *(he looked hungrier)*

Daniel: “I went through next door. The shelves are bare.”

Elena had it out with Daniel: “Daniel, I said no. You went.”

Daniel to Elena: “I'm back, aren't I, Mom?”

Daniel said he would go further along the street tonight: “I'm going further along tonight. I've decided.”

Elena did not forbid Daniel this time: “You know what I think, Daniel. Be back before light.”

Leo told Daniel to stay home: “Daniel, wait a day. One day.”

**The day's work.** Leo counted the stores; Mara saw to the water; Mara kept watch; Leo went round the boards. Daniel and Elena slept through it.

**Afternoon.** Daniel to Elena: “Thanks, Mom.” Leo: “I counted twice: 4 portions left.” Mara: “Nobody. I didn't see anybody.”

**Evening.** Leo set out a small meal: “Small shares today. Eat slowly.” *(taking it off Elena's hands)* Everyone ate.

**Night.** Elena sat up by the front door; Leo and Mara slept.

Daniel waited until the house was quiet and went out along the street. *(meant to go further)*

Leo told Daniel not to go: “Daniel. Don't.”

Daniel went all the same: “Go back to bed. I'll be back before light.”

Elena told Daniel not to go: “Daniel. Come back to bed.”

Daniel went all the same: “I said I would go. I'm going.”

Daniel was not back by morning.

## Day 6

3 portions on the shelf. Daniel is gone. Elena woke low: hunger and Daniel not back and no sleep. Leo woke low: hunger and Daniel not back. Mara woke low: Daniel not back.

**Morning.** Mara set out a small meal: “Half each. That's all there is.” *(taking it off Elena's hands)* Everyone ate.

Mara stood at the gap in the boards, watching the street for Daniel.

Leo said he would go out tonight to look for Daniel: “I'll go along the street tonight and look for Daniel. Someone should.”

Elena told Leo to stay home: “I will not lose another one to that street, Leo.” *(Daniel did not come back)*

Leo comforted Mara: “Mara. We do not know anything yet.” *(someone has not come back)*

Mara told Leo to stay home: “Leo, no. Daniel said the same thing. Please.” *(Daniel did not come back)*

Mara: “I keep listening for the door.”

Someone knocked at the front door.

Mara looked through the gap in the boards and saw a woman of about forty on the step.

Leo sent them away: “We have nothing to give. I am sorry.”

**The day's work.** Mara saw to the water; Leo counted the stores; Mara kept watch for Daniel; Leo went round the boards. Elena slept through it.

Mara sat with Leo. *(someone has not come back)*

**Afternoon.** Mara took a portion from the shelf and ate it. *(starving)*

Elena sat with Leo. *(someone has not come back)*

Leo: “I counted: 1 and a quarter portions on the shelf.” Mara: “Whoever it was, they're gone.”

Elena had it out with Mara: “We eat at the table, Mara. All of us or none.”

Mara to Elena: “I couldn't help it, Mom. I couldn't.”

**Evening.** Leo set out a small meal: “Half shares. It keeps us going longer.” Elena and Leo ate.

Mara gave her share to Leo: “Take it, Leo. I can't eat anyway.” *(he looked hungrier; Leo spoke kindly to her, today)*

Leo to Mara: “That mattered, Mara. Thank you.” Mara to Leo: “It's nothing, Leo.”

**Night.** Elena sat up by the front door; Mara lay awake.

Leo waited until the house was quiet and got up to go out. *(meant to go further)*

Elena told Leo not to go: “Leo. Take your hand off that door.”

Leo went back to bed: “Fine. I'll stay.”

## Day 7

Half a portion on the shelf. Daniel is gone. Elena woke on edge: a night on watch and yesterday's quarrel. Leo woke low: a bad night and Daniel still gone. Mara woke low: being told off yesterday and Daniel still gone.

**Morning.** Mara set out a small meal: “It's small shares. I'm sorry.” Elena and Leo ate.

Mara gave her share to Leo: “Have mine, Leo. Go on.” *(he looked hungrier; Leo spoke kindly to her, day 6)*

Leo stood at the gap in the boards, watching the street for Daniel.

Elena sat with Leo. *(someone has not come back)*

Elena said she would go out tonight to look for Daniel: “Daniel is out there. I am not sitting here another night.”

Leo told Elena to stay home: “One of us is already out there, Mom. Not two.” *(Daniel did not come back)*

Mara told Elena to stay home: “Daniel is not back, Mom! You can't!” *(Daniel did not come back)*

Leo to Mara: “Thank you, Mara.” Mara to Leo: “You'd do it for me, Leo.”

**The day's work.** Leo counted the stores; Mara saw to the water; Mara kept watch for Daniel; Leo went round the boards. Elena slept through it.

Mara sat with Leo. *(someone has not come back)*

**Afternoon.** Mara: “The street's empty. Nothing moved.” Leo: “Nothing on the shelf. I looked twice.”

Leo comforted Elena: “Daniel may be waiting for dark, Mom.” *(someone has not come back)*

Elena to Leo: “I know, Leo. I know.”

**Evening.** Elena handed out the water, a cup each: “The shelf is empty. We drink, and we sit together.” Everyone drank.

# The week, seed 1

Elena, 42, the mother. Daniel, 25. Leo, 21. Mara, 17. The house is shut; the taps are dry; a can is missing from the shelf.

## Day 1

16 portions on the shelf. Daniel and Leo woke on edge. Elena woke low.

**Morning.** Leo said the water would go out by the cup from now on: “We should measure the water. A cup at a time.”

Elena asked who took the can: “A can is gone from the shelf. I want to know who took it.”

Mara said it was not her: “I didn't take it, I swear.”

Elena set out the meal: “Sit down, all of you. We eat first.” Everyone ate.

Leo said it was not him: “I did not take it. I would say if I had.”

Daniel said it was not him: “I didn't take any can.”

**The day's work.** Mara saw to the water; Daniel went round the boards; Elena looked after Daniel; Leo kept watch. Daniel cut his hand on the boards.

Daniel to Elena: “Mom. I owe you for that.” Elena to Daniel: “It is nothing, Daniel.” Leo to Daniel: “Daniel, did you sleep at all?” Daniel to Leo: “Leave it, Leo. I'm up.”

**Afternoon.** Leo: “I saw nobody out there.”

Leo searched the kitchen for the can.

**Evening.** Elena set out the meal: “Come to the table. A share each.” Everyone ate.

**Night.** Daniel, Elena and Mara lay awake; Leo slept.

## Day 2

12 portions on the shelf. Daniel woke low: a cut hand. Elena woke on edge: a bad night. Mara woke on edge: a bad night. Leo woke warm.

**Morning.** Leo to Daniel: “How is it today, Daniel?”

Elena set out the meal: “Sit down, all of you. We eat first.” Everyone ate.

Mara: “I hardly slept.”

**The day's work.** Mara looked after Daniel; Elena saw to the water; Leo kept watch.

Daniel to Mara: “Mara. I owe you for that.” Mara to Daniel: “You'd do it for me, Daniel.”

**Afternoon.** Leo: “Nothing moved in the street.”

**Evening.** Elena set out a small meal: “I am cutting the shares. 10 portions, and nothing coming.” Everyone ate.

Mara: “That's all? For the whole day?”

**Night.** Daniel, Leo and Mara slept; Elena lay awake. Daniel was well again.

## Day 3

9 portions on the shelf. Daniel woke warm: yesterday's kindness. Elena woke on edge: a bad night. Leo woke warm. Mara woke on edge.

**Morning.** Daniel: “Yesterday helped.”

Elena set out a small meal: “Half shares. Eat slowly.” Everyone ate.

Daniel went to the brothers room.

**The day's work.** Elena saw to the water; Mara counted the stores; Leo kept watch; Daniel went round the boards.

**Afternoon.** Leo: “I saw nobody out there.” Mara: “I counted. 8 portions.”

Daniel took Elena aside.

Daniel owned up, to Elena alone: “I took the can. I ate it. There.” *(what is on him)*

Elena had it out with Daniel: “You said no to my face, Daniel.” *(Daniel took the can and denied it to her face, today)*

Leo went out the back into the rain and set the bin under the drainpipe.

Daniel to Elena: “I have no excuse, Mom.”

**Evening.** Elena set out a small meal: “Small shares again. It has to last.” Everyone ate.

Elena told the others who took the can: “It was Daniel. He has told me so.”

Mara had it out with Daniel: “I swore it wasn't me, Daniel. And you just stood there!” *(Daniel took the can and denied it to her face, today)*

Daniel to Mara: “I was ashamed, Mara.”

Leo had it out with Daniel: “You said it was not you, Daniel. I heard you.” *(Daniel took the can and denied it to his face, today)*

Daniel to Leo: “I have no excuse, Leo.”

Daniel said he would go next door tonight: “The can was me. I go next door tonight.”

Leo said nobody should go out: “Nobody should go out tonight. It is not worth one night.”

Elena said nobody should go out: “Nobody leaves this house tonight.”

**Night.** Elena sat up by the front door; Leo slept; Mara lay awake.

Daniel waited until the house was quiet and went out to the neighbour's house. *(meant to go)*

Leo told Daniel not to go: “Daniel. Think. Then go back to bed.”

Daniel went all the same: “I said I would go. I'm going.”

Elena told Daniel not to go: “Daniel, no. Not in the dark.”

Daniel went all the same: “Go back to bed. I'll be back before light.”

Mara heard the front door and stayed in bed.

Daniel was back with water.

Daniel no longer meant to go next door: next door is empty.

## Day 4

7 portions on the shelf. Daniel woke on edge: hunger and being told off yesterday and no sleep. Elena woke on edge: a night on watch and yesterday's quarrel. Leo woke on edge: yesterday's quarrel. Mara woke on edge: a bad night and yesterday's quarrel.

**Morning.** Mara set out a small meal: “Half each. That's all there is.” *(taking it off Elena's hands)* Everyone ate.

Daniel: “I went through next door. The shelves are bare.”

Elena had it out with Daniel: “Daniel, I said no. You went.” *(Daniel went out after she said no, yesterday)*

Daniel to Elena: “I know what you said, Mom. I went anyway.”

Daniel said he would go further along the street tonight: “I'll go along the street tonight. It should be me.”

Elena did not forbid Daniel this time: “I will not say it twice, Daniel. Come back.”

Leo did not forbid Daniel this time: “Then go early and be back before light, Daniel.”

Elena lay down and slept.

**The day's work.** Elena saw to the water; Leo kept watch; Mara counted the stores; Elena went round the boards. Daniel slept through it.

**Afternoon.** Mara: “6 portions left. I counted twice.” Leo: “Nothing moved in the street.”

**Evening.** Daniel set out a small meal: “Half each. I'll put it out.” *(making up for the can)* Everyone ate.

**Night.** Elena sat up by the front door; Leo slept; Mara lay awake.

Daniel waited until the house was quiet and went out along the street. *(meant to go further)*

Elena saw Daniel go and said nothing.

Mara heard the front door and stayed in bed.

Daniel was not back by morning.

## Day 5

5 portions on the shelf. Daniel is gone. Elena woke low: Daniel not back and no sleep. Leo woke low: hunger and Daniel not back. Mara woke low: Daniel not back.

**Morning.** Mara set out a small meal: “It's small shares. I'm sorry.” Everyone ate.

Leo stood at the gap in the boards, watching the street for Daniel.

Elena comforted Mara: “We keep the door ready, Mara. That is all we can do.” *(someone has not come back)*

Mara to Elena: “I keep listening for the door, Mom.” Mara: “The bed's still empty.”

Leo said he would go out tonight to look for Daniel: “Someone has to look for Daniel. It should be me.”

Elena told Leo to stay home: “Daniel went. Daniel is not here. No, Leo.” *(Daniel did not come back)*

Mara told Leo to stay home: “Daniel is not back, Leo! You can't!” *(Daniel did not come back)*

**The day's work.** Leo saw to the water; Mara counted the stores; Leo kept watch for Daniel; Mara went round the boards. Elena slept through it.

Leo comforted Mara: “Mara. We do not know anything yet.” *(someone has not come back)*

Mara to Leo: “Don't say it, Leo.”

**Afternoon.** Leo: “I saw nobody out there.”

Mara sat with Elena. *(someone has not come back)*

Mara: “4 and a quarter portions left. I counted twice.”

**Evening.** Mara set out a small meal: “Half each. That's all there is.” Everyone ate.

**Night.** Elena sat up by the front door; Mara lay awake.

Leo waited until the house was quiet and went out along the street. *(meant to go further)*

Elena told Leo not to go: “Leo. Take your hand off that door.”

Leo went all the same: “I have to go. Go back to bed.”

Mara heard the front door and stayed in bed.

Leo was back shaken, with nothing.

Leo no longer meant to go further along the street: it nearly went wrong out there.

## Day 6

3 and a half portions on the shelf. Daniel is gone. Elena woke low: hunger and Daniel still gone and no sleep. Leo woke low: a night out and hunger and Daniel still gone. Mara woke low: Daniel still gone.

**Morning.** Leo set out a small meal: “Half shares. It keeps us going longer.” *(taking it off Elena's hands)* Everyone ate.

Elena stood at the gap in the boards, watching the street for Daniel.

Leo: “It nearly went wrong out there. I came back with nothing.”

Mara sat with Elena. *(someone has not come back)*

Elena had it out with Leo: “I told you not to go, Leo. You went all the same.” *(Leo went out after she said no, yesterday)*

Leo to Elena: “I know what you said, Mom. I went anyway.”

**The day's work.** Mara saw to the water. Elena and Leo slept through it.

**Afternoon.** Mara took a portion from the shelf and ate it. *(starving)*

Leo had it out with Mara: “Mara, that portion was everyone's.” *(Mara ate in front of him, today)*

Mara to Leo: “I was so hungry, Leo. I'm sorry.”

Leo comforted Elena: “Daniel may be waiting for dark, Mom.” *(someone has not come back)*

Someone knocked at the front door.

Elena looked through the gap in the boards and saw a woman of about fifty on the step.

Elena sent them away: “There is nothing here for you. Go on.”

**Evening.** Elena set out a small meal: “This is what there is today. Sit down.” Elena and Leo ate.

Mara gave her share to Leo: “Have mine, Leo. Go on.” *(he looked hungrier)*

**Night.** Elena, Leo and Mara slept.

## Day 7

1 portion on the shelf. Daniel is gone. Elena woke warm: yesterday's kindness. Leo woke warm: being given food. Mara woke low: being told off yesterday.

**Morning.** Mara set out a small meal: “It's small shares. I'm sorry.” Everyone ate.

Leo to Mara: “Thank you, Mara.” Mara to Leo: “You'd do it for me, Leo.”

**The day's work.** Leo saw to the water; Mara counted the stores; Elena kept watch for Daniel; Mara went round the boards.

**Afternoon.** Elena: “Nobody out there. I watched the whole time.” Mara: “I counted. a quarter of a portion.”

**Evening.** Leo set out a small meal: “Small shares today. Eat slowly.” Everyone ate.

Leo said he would go further along the street tonight: “I'll go along the street tonight. I am the one most likely to get back.” *(new: someone is starving)*

Elena told Leo to stay home: “I will not lose another one to that street, Leo.” *(Daniel did not come back)*

Mara told Leo to stay home: “Leo, no. Daniel said the same thing. Please.” *(Daniel did not come back)*

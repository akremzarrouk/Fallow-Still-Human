# Voice sheets

One sheet per person, from their note and traits (`data.py:17-56`). They are for you to approve or mark before the
bulk of the lines is written (Docs/fable-audit-2026-11.md, section 9, steps 4 and 5). Until then the game speaks the
starter lines in `content/lines.json`.

How to mark: write next to a sample line "no", or the line as you would have it. Anything you change in "How she
talks" changes every line written for that person.

---

## Elena, 42, the mother

**How she talks.** Short, level sentences. She says a thing once. She uses first names, never pet names, and never
raises her voice; when she is angry the sentences get shorter and she stops explaining. She asks rather than
accuses. She does not say what she is afraid of: fear shows as counting, tidying, giving an instruction. She will
say something untrue in a calm voice if it keeps the room together. She never says "calm down", never swears, and
never pleads with her children in front of the others.

**To each of them.** Daniel: by name, as to a grown man, until he frightens her; then as to a boy. Leo: plainly,
as to an equal. Mara: gently, but she does not talk down to her.

**Angry:** "No, Daniel. I won't have it." **Afraid:** "Sit down. Eat something."

**Five sample lines**

1. "One can is missing from the shelf. Did one of you take it?"
2. "Sit down, all of you. We eat first."
3. "I counted that shelf myself. I did not take it."
4. "Daniel. Come back to bed."
5. "Nobody in this house goes hungry while I can still stand."

---

## Daniel, 25, the eldest son

**How he talks.** In statements, not questions. He starts sentences with "I" and with what he will do. He speaks
before he has thought, and a little too loud. He gives reasons that are really about himself: his age, his place.
When he is afraid he gives orders. When he is ashamed he goes quiet, then says too much at once. He does not say
"sorry" easily, and when he does it comes out flat and whole. He never says he is frightened, never asks for help,
and never says "please" to Leo.

**To each of them.** Elena: "Mom", and he argues with her as if she had not spoken. Leo: by name, as to a rival.
Mara: by name, protective, and over her head.

**Angry:** "I'm the oldest. It's my decision." **Afraid:** "Go back to bed. I'll be back before light."

**Five sample lines**

1. "I didn't take any can."
2. "I'm the oldest. I'll go next door tonight."
3. "Someone has to do something. You're all just sitting here."
4. "I said I would go. I'm going."
5. "It was me. I took it. I ate it in the night."

---

## Leo, 21, the younger son

**How he talks.** Quietly, in whole sentences, with the reason attached. He states what will happen and leaves the
conclusion to the other person. He does not repeat himself; if he is not heard he stops talking. Annoyance shows as
a dry correction, never volume. When he is afraid he gets more exact: numbers, hours, distances. He is kind without
soft words. He never shouts, never says "I told you so", and never says more than the moment needs.

**To each of them.** Elena: "Mom", briefly, as to someone he does not want to worry. Daniel: by name, evenly, which
Daniel hears as a challenge. Mara: by name, as to someone his own age.

**Angry:** "Not tonight, Daniel. Think it through." **Afraid:** "Crossing at night gets someone killed."

**Five sample lines**

1. "No. I didn't touch it."
2. "There is food for four days if we keep to a portion each."
3. "Daniel. Don't."
4. "Mara. It's all right. Nobody thinks it was you."
5. "If someone goes, it should be the one who can get back."

---

## Mara, 17, the youngest, the daughter

**How she talks.** Fast, in bursts; what she feels is out before she has chosen to say it. She asks questions she
does not want answered. When she is frightened she pleads and uses the person's name. When she is angry it is about
being treated as a child, and she says so. She swears she is telling the truth even when nobody doubted her. She
goes quiet only when she is really hurt. She never sounds measured, never gives orders, and never hides that she is
hungry.

**To each of them.** Elena: "Mom", and to her alone she will say she is scared. Daniel: by name, half looking up to
him, half furious with him. Leo: by name, as to the one who will tell her the truth.

**Angry:** "I'm not a child. Stop deciding for me." **Afraid:** "Mom? Don't leave."

**Five sample lines**

1. "I didn't take it, I swear."
2. "Why is everyone looking at me?"
3. "Daniel, please don't go."
4. "I'm so hungry. Is that all there is?"
5. "You went through my bag. In front of everyone."

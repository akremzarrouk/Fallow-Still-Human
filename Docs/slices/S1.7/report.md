# Slice S1.7 report: can yesterday change tomorrow?

Date: 2026-09-16. Status: complete, stopped for review. **No mechanism was added and no rule,
number or line of `Fallow.Core` was changed.** The whole slice is a scenario, an instrument and
a set of measurements on the architecture exactly as S1.6 left it.
Reproduce with `./run-tests.sh`.

**In short.** Yes, and less than you would want. A past event changes what a later, otherwise
identical event *means*, and through that what it feels like and what the person wants, by a
path that reconstructs completely from the trace and that needed no new code. It requires the
person to have perceived it, and it is about the person who did it rather than a mood. But it
is a **step function, not a dial**: eleven earlier slights change nothing whatsoever, and the
twelfth changes everything at once. Only one of four kinds of later situation is sensitive to
history at all. The changed want changed the *act* in two of twenty-seven circumstances, and
over a whole morning the effect sits **below the seed-noise floor** for all four people. The
causal pipeline is longitudinal as far as motivation and dies at deliberation.

---

## 1. Why the suggested scenario was not used

The brief proposed an accusation, a defence, and a later request, measuring whether the subject
complies. Three things in the code made that a measurement of nothing, and they are worth
stating because each is a real defect:

1. **There is no compliance.** Seven actions exist and none of them is agreeing or refusing to
   do what somebody asked. Measuring it would have required inventing the action, the want and
   the proposals, and the result would have been a reading of what I had just written.
2. **The record it would rest on is inert.** `p02` already authors `searched_my_things` into
   Mara's ledger about Daniel at weight 0.8, and no rule anywhere reads it. Five of the eight
   ledger entry kinds are written by the data and read by nothing.
3. **Nothing in the pipeline ever writes a ledger entry.** All fourteen ledger writes in the
   data are authored onto hand-written events. Relationship history is authored, not simulated.

So the experiment used the one longitudinal loop that is genuinely shipped and had never been
tested: a reading of `disrespect` nudges a belief about the person, and later interpretation
rules read that belief back. Every link is a general rule already in the data.

## 2. What was actually demonstrated

**The chain runs, end to end, and it is real.** One person, one later event built identically in
every arm, and nothing different between arms but what happened to her before:

| | C: never happened | T: happened, she saw it | U: happened, she did not see it | P: happened, someone else does it now |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| belief `treats_me_like_a_child` | 0.000 | 0.784 | **0.000** | 0.784 (about him, not the actor) |
| read the later event as | threat 0.900 | **disrespect 0.919** | threat 0.900 | threat 0.900 |
| it stirred | anxiety 0.688, fear 0.555 | **hurt 0.565, frustration 0.402, anger 0.313, shame 0.161** | anxiety 0.688, fear 0.555 | anxiety 0.688, fear 0.555 |
| wants `keep_peace` | 0.964 | **0.825** | 0.964 | 0.964 |
| wants `restore_standing` | 0.053 | **0.273** | 0.053 | 0.053 |
| wants `find_out` | 0.789 | **0.480** | 0.789 | 0.789 |
| chose | go_to->kitchen | go_to->kitchen | go_to->kitchen | go_to->kitchen |
| for | keep_peace | **get_food** | keep_peace | keep_peace |
| resolution | too close to call | **clear** | too close to call | too close to call |

The same search of her things is a threat to a woman with no history with him and a slight to
the same woman who has watched him do it before. Nothing about her, the room, the people or the
event differs.

- **It travels through perception.** In arm U the event happened in the world and she was not
  there; her belief is 0.000 and every number matches the control. World truth does not leak.
- **It is about a person.** In arm P she carries the full belief about Daniel and Leo does the
  searching; she reads it exactly as the control does. This is not a mood and not a hostility
  meter.
- **The whole path reconstructs from the trace.** The decision rests on 73 records, 60 of them
  belonging to the twelve earlier days. The trace shows the belief climbing 0.00, 0.12, 0.23,
  0.32, 0.40, 0.47, 0.54 across those days, each step resting on its own perception of its own
  event. Nothing in the path is a stored conclusion.

**The shape of the effect is a step, and that is the strongest evidence that it is not a
patch.** Sweeping the history from none to fourteen earlier times:

| Earlier times | 0 | ... | 10 | **11** | 12 | 13 | 14 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| belief | 0.000 | climbing every step | 0.721 | **0.755** | 0.784 | 0.810 | 0.833 |
| read as | threat | threat | threat | **disrespect** | disrespect | disrespect | disrespect |
| `restore_standing` | 0.053 | 0.053 | 0.053 | **0.273** | 0.273 | 0.273 | 0.273 |

Below the flip there is **one distinct downstream state across all eleven of them**, to nine
decimal places, while the belief moves at every step. A hidden modifier would have moved
behaviour a little each time. This does not, because the only route from a belief about a
person to a feeling is through changing which reading wins.

**The two kinds of belief behave oppositely on the same instrument.** `supplies_short` is learned
by the same machinery from the same kind of event, and is read by a motivation rule rather than
only by interpretation. Swept over the same range it moves `guard_supplies` at every step
(0.575 to 0.935, continuously) with no flat band. So the flat band is a property of the
architecture, not of the instrument.

**Time does not wear it off.** The same history at one day, two days, three days and thirty days
lands identically. Nothing decays a belief. This is the mirror image of the never-fading memory
S1.6 found, and it belongs in the same family of defects rather than being a success.

## 3. What failed

1. **The changed want almost never changes the act.** Across twenty-seven immediate
   circumstances (three rooms, three companions, three levels of hunger) the act differed in
   **2**. The reason behind the act differed in 7. In the other twenty the same thing was done
   for the same reason.
2. **Over a morning it is below the noise floor.** With the history written into the backstory
   and mornings run on seeds no test had used, against two same-world seed pools:

   | Who | Effect | Noise | Effect / noise |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | daniel | 0.008 | 0.009 | 0.8 |
   | elena | 0.029 | 0.034 | 0.9 |
   | leo | 0.004 | 0.006 | 0.6 |
   | mara | 0.028 | 0.035 | 0.8 |

   Nobody is above 1.0, let alone the 1.5 the S1.1 measure calls a real effect. Twelve
   deliberate slights, a flipped reading and four changed wants do not produce a morning you
   could tell from the dice.
3. **Only one of four kinds of later situation is sensitive at all.** History moved the
   interpretation *weight* in three of four, and moved the *outcome* in one. Being watched is
   read nearly two and a half times as darkly and nothing follows, because only one rule fires
   on it and the extra weight has nothing to beat. A demand is read more darkly and nothing
   follows, because that reading already won. Being read more strongly is not being read
   differently, and only the second has any consequence.
4. **Eleven times is a great deal of history** for a threshold. Nothing chose that number, and
   it is not tunable without changing shipped rule weights, but it means the mechanism is far
   less sensitive than a person is.
5. **The one behavioural difference that showed up on the bench sat inside the ambiguity band.**
   In the still-felt arm of the diagnosis, both arms' choices were "too close to call", so the
   difference such left-over feeling makes there is settled by the seed, not by the history.

## 4. What was assumed

- **The backstory is not run.** Each arm's people have only the history that arm gives them, so
  the independent variable is the only difference. This makes the bench clean and makes it
  unlike the shipped scenario, where everybody starts with three days behind them.
- **The lag is long enough that nothing is still felt** (forty fades, from the diagnosis
  measurement). Without that, the arms differ through left-over mood, which is a real effect
  but a short-lived one and not longitudinal. Separating those two was necessary and is the
  reason the first version of the diagnosis assertion failed.
- **The subject is answerable for what is missing**, so that two readings of the later event are
  live at once. Without that the competing reading scores zero and no amount of history can flip
  anything. This is a strong assumption and section 5 treats it as the main overfitting risk.
- **Twelve earlier events on twelve separate days** is not a claim about people. It is the
  smallest number above the flip the sweep found.
- **The morning arm writes the history into the backstory**, which changes what everybody else
  experiences too, not only the subject. It is an honest counterfactual but a broad one.

## 5. What could be overfitting

Stated plainly, because it is the part I would attack if someone else wrote this.

- **The subject was chosen for the boundary.** Elena reads a search as `disrespect` at 0.610
  before any history, against a competing `threat` at 0.900. Mara, on the same event, reads
  disrespect at 1.04 and would never flip however much history she had, because her reading
  already wins. The mechanism only does anything for a person whose two readings start close.
  I did not pick her trait values, but I did pick her, and a different subject would have shown
  no effect at all.
- **The competing reading is supplied by guilt**, which is a different past event. The flip is
  really a contest between two histories, not between history and nothing. That is arguably
  more interesting, but it means the clean claim "history versus no history" is not quite what
  was measured.
- **The later event is the same kind of event as the history.** He goes through her things, and
  later he goes through her things. A wider class was tested (section 3, item 3) and only this
  one moved, so the mechanism may be narrower than "history changes how you read people".

## 6. What was not tested

- Any personality configuration other than the shipped four. The window's width as a function of
  traits and values is unmeasured.
- Whether the effect appears for a subject whose competing reading comes from something other
  than guilt.
- Speech. Nobody speaks in this slice, so the `support` route that lowers `does_not_respect_me`
  was never exercised in anger, and no reading can be answered by anybody saying anything.
- Whether a flipped reading would change the act in a scenario with more to do. S1.6 already
  found people standing still most of a silent morning because nothing is worth doing; a want
  with no distinctive outlet cannot show.
- The `B` disposition model and the S1.6 fixes. Everything here ran on the shipped rules (`A`,
  `means: want`, untimed scripted events).

## 6b. The predictions, checked

Committed in `held-out-predictions.md` before any S1.7 code existed (`1d59e43`).

| | Prediction | Result |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | T perceives it, keeps it as `disrespect`, both tendency beliefs rise; U's belief does not move | **held** (T 0.784 and 0.914; U 0.000 and 0.000) |
| P2 | With little history the meaning is unchanged and every downstream number is identical to 1e-9 while the weight differs | **held** (one earlier time against six: weight 0.653 against 0.819, downstream identical) |
| P3 | A history strength exists at which the meaning flips, and everything downstream changes together | **held** (at eleven; emotions, wants and the ranking all move at once) |
| P4 | `supplies_short` moves its want continuously, with no flat band | **held** (0.575 to 0.935 across nine steps) |
| P5 | Arm P matches the control, not the treatment | **held** (threat 0.900, identical to C) |
| P6 | The effect is identical at every lag, because no belief decays | **held** (one day to thirty days, identical) |
| P7 | `observe` is weight-sensitive but cannot flip; `search` can flip; the `demand` cannot learn | **MISSED on the third part.** The first two held. The demand *does* learn, through `corrected_by_a_junior_with_people_watching` reading `does_not_respect_me`, and is read from 1.445 to 1.811. I had only looked at `being_ordered_about`, whose belief nothing writes. Nothing followed anyway, but for a different reason than predicted: `disrespect` was already winning. |
| P8 | With one earlier time, a whole morning is identical on a shared seed | **MISSED** (identical in 9 of 10 seeds, not 10) |

Six held, one missed outright, one missed in a third of itself. Both misses are in the same
direction: I underestimated how often history moves an interpretation *weight*, and
overestimated how reliably a weight change stays invisible. The second miss matters, because a
single earlier event changing one morning in ten is the only sign in this slice that the
categorical account is not the whole story, and it is not explained. It is most likely the
left-over-feeling channel rather than the belief one, but that was not isolated.

| The effect is categorical, not a graded hidden modifier | **PROVEN** (eleven identical downstream states, then a step) |

## 8. Recommendation

**MODIFY**, and the modification is not in this slice's subject matter.

| Component | Decision |
|---|---|
| The S1.7 instrument (`S17Measures`, the arms, the response curve) | **KEEP.** It measures where causal influence dies, which is the question worth asking of every later slice. |
| The belief loop as the longitudinal carrier | **KEEP.** It works, it is general, it is traceable, and it cost nothing. |
| Treating "the character remembers" as evidence of anything | **ABANDON.** This slice measured the gap directly: eleven memories that change nothing, and a twelfth that changes everything. |
| The categorical channel as the *only* route for history about people | **MODIFY**, next slice. See below. |
| Beliefs that never decay | **MODIFY**, and recognise it as the same defect as S1.6's never-fading memory. |
| The ledger | **REBUILD** when relationships matter. It is authored, mostly unread, and cannot support any claim. |

**The missing general mechanism, named as the brief requires.** Interpretation produces a
winning meaning *and a weight*, and throws the weight away. Everything downstream is therefore
blind to how clearly something was read. That is why history has to flip a category to matter at
all, and it is why a person who has been slighted eleven times feels exactly what a stranger
feels. The general fix is that **how strongly a reading won should reach how hard it lands** —
appraisal already takes an intensity scale (`reach × attention`), and the margin by which a
reading beat its alternatives is the obvious third factor. That is one multiplication, it is
general, it names no scenario, and it would convert the categorical channel into a graded one
without adding a modifier, a decay constant or a relationship meter.

It must not be done inside this slice, and I have not done it. It changes every number in every
existing trace, so it needs its own diagnosis, its own held-out predictions committed first, and
its own regression run. The test that would distinguish it from a patch is already built: the
response curve in section 2 should stop being flat.

**What the next experiment should test.** Whether making the margin of a reading reach its
intensity turns the step into a slope, and whether *that* reaches the act and survives a morning
above the noise. If it does not, the failure is in deliberation rather than in motivation, and
the honest conclusion will be that a silent house with seven actions cannot express a changed
inner life, which is a scenario problem and not an architecture one.

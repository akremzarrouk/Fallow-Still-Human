## report.md

### Slice S1.7 report: can yesterday change tomorrow?

Date: 2026-09-16. Status: complete, stopped for review. **No mechanism was added and no rule,
number or line of `Fallow.Core` was changed.** The whole slice is a scenario, an instrument and
a set of measurements on the architecture exactly as S1.6 left it.
Reproduce with `./run-tests.sh`.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `diagnosis.md` | What can carry a past event into a later decision, in words (by hand) |
| `diagnosis-before.md` | Generated by `S17DiagnosisTests`, frozen |
| `diagnosis-current.md` | The same tests on the current code |
| `held-out-predictions.md` | The hypothesis, why the suggested scenario was rejected, and eight predictions, committed before any S1.7 code (`1d59e43`) |
| `experiment-results.md` | The eight measurements, generated by `S17ExperimentTests` |
| `S1.7-all.md` | All of the above merged into one document |

**In short.** Yes, and less than you would want. A past event changes what a later, otherwise
identical event *means*, and through that what it feels like and what the person wants, by a
path that reconstructs completely from the trace and that needed no new code. It requires the
person to have perceived it, and it is about the person who did it rather than a mood. But it
is a **step function, not a dial**: eleven earlier slights change nothing whatsoever, and the
twelfth changes everything at once. Only one of four kinds of later situation is sensitive to
history at all. The changed want changed the *act* in two of twenty-seven circumstances, and
over a whole morning the effect sits **below the seed-noise floor** for all four people. The
causal pipeline is longitudinal as far as motivation and dies at deliberation.

---

#### 1. Why the suggested scenario was not used

The brief proposed an accusation, a defence, and a later request, measuring whether the subject
complies. Three things in the code made that a measurement of nothing, and they are worth
stating because each is a real defect:

1. **There is no compliance.** Seven actions exist and none of them is agreeing or refusing to
   do what somebody asked. Measuring it would have required inventing the action, the want and
   the proposals, and the result would have been a reading of what I had just written.
2. **The record it would rest on is inert.** `p02` already authors `searched_my_things` into
   Mara's ledger about Daniel at weight 0.8, and no rule anywhere reads it. Five of the eight
   ledger entry kinds are written by the data and read by nothing.
3. **Nothing in the pipeline ever writes a ledger entry.** All fourteen ledger writes in the
   data are authored onto hand-written events. Relationship history is authored, not simulated.

So the experiment used the one longitudinal loop that is genuinely shipped and had never been
tested: a reading of `disrespect` nudges a belief about the person, and later interpretation
rules read that belief back. Every link is a general rule already in the data.

#### 2. What was actually demonstrated

**The chain runs, end to end, and it is real.** One person, one later event built identically in
every arm, and nothing different between arms but what happened to her before:

| | C: never happened | T: happened, she saw it | U: happened, she did not see it | P: happened, someone else does it now |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| belief `treats_me_like_a_child` | 0.000 | 0.784 | **0.000** | 0.784 (about him, not the actor) |
| read the later event as | threat 0.900 | **disrespect 0.919** | threat 0.900 | threat 0.900 |
| it stirred | anxiety 0.688, fear 0.555 | **hurt 0.565, frustration 0.402, anger 0.313, shame 0.161** | anxiety 0.688, fear 0.555 | anxiety 0.688, fear 0.555 |
| wants `keep_peace` | 0.964 | **0.825** | 0.964 | 0.964 |
| wants `restore_standing` | 0.053 | **0.273** | 0.053 | 0.053 |
| wants `find_out` | 0.789 | **0.480** | 0.789 | 0.789 |
| chose | go_to->kitchen | go_to->kitchen | go_to->kitchen | go_to->kitchen |
| for | keep_peace | **get_food** | keep_peace | keep_peace |
| resolution | too close to call | **clear** | too close to call | too close to call |

The same search of her things is a threat to a woman with no history with him and a slight to
the same woman who has watched him do it before. Nothing about her, the room, the people or the
event differs.

- **It travels through perception.** In arm U the event happened in the world and she was not
  there; her belief is 0.000 and every number matches the control. World truth does not leak.
- **It is about a person.** In arm P she carries the full belief about Daniel and Leo does the
  searching; she reads it exactly as the control does. This is not a mood and not a hostility
  meter.
- **The whole path reconstructs from the trace.** The decision rests on 73 records, 60 of them
  belonging to the twelve earlier days. The trace shows the belief climbing 0.00, 0.12, 0.23,
  0.32, 0.40, 0.47, 0.54 across those days, each step resting on its own perception of its own
  event. Nothing in the path is a stored conclusion.

**The shape of the effect is a step, and that is the strongest evidence that it is not a
patch.** Sweeping the history from none to fourteen earlier times:

| Earlier times | 0 | ... | 10 | **11** | 12 | 13 | 14 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| belief | 0.000 | climbing every step | 0.721 | **0.755** | 0.784 | 0.810 | 0.833 |
| read as | threat | threat | threat | **disrespect** | disrespect | disrespect | disrespect |
| `restore_standing` | 0.053 | 0.053 | 0.053 | **0.273** | 0.273 | 0.273 | 0.273 |

Below the flip there is **one distinct downstream state across all eleven of them**, to nine
decimal places, while the belief moves at every step. A hidden modifier would have moved
behaviour a little each time. This does not, because the only route from a belief about a
person to a feeling is through changing which reading wins.

**The two kinds of belief behave oppositely on the same instrument.** `supplies_short` is learned
by the same machinery from the same kind of event, and is read by a motivation rule rather than
only by interpretation. Swept over the same range it moves `guard_supplies` at every step
(0.575 to 0.935, continuously) with no flat band. So the flat band is a property of the
architecture, not of the instrument.

**Time does not wear it off.** The same history at one day, two days, three days and thirty days
lands identically. Nothing decays a belief. This is the mirror image of the never-fading memory
S1.6 found, and it belongs in the same family of defects rather than being a success.

#### 3. What failed

1. **The changed want almost never changes the act.** Across twenty-seven immediate
   circumstances (three rooms, three companions, three levels of hunger) the act differed in
   **2**. The reason behind the act differed in 7. In the other twenty the same thing was done
   for the same reason.
2. **Over a morning it is below the noise floor.** With the history written into the backstory
   and mornings run on seeds no test had used, against two same-world seed pools:

   | Who | Effect | Noise | Effect / noise |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | daniel | 0.008 | 0.009 | 0.8 |
   | elena | 0.029 | 0.034 | 0.9 |
   | leo | 0.004 | 0.006 | 0.6 |
   | mara | 0.028 | 0.035 | 0.8 |

   Nobody is above 1.0, let alone the 1.5 the S1.1 measure calls a real effect. Twelve
   deliberate slights, a flipped reading and four changed wants do not produce a morning you
   could tell from the dice.
3. **Only one of four kinds of later situation is sensitive at all.** History moved the
   interpretation *weight* in three of four, and moved the *outcome* in one. Being watched is
   read nearly two and a half times as darkly and nothing follows, because only one rule fires
   on it and the extra weight has nothing to beat. A demand is read more darkly and nothing
   follows, because that reading already won. Being read more strongly is not being read
   differently, and only the second has any consequence.
4. **Eleven times is a great deal of history** for a threshold. Nothing chose that number, and
   it is not tunable without changing shipped rule weights, but it means the mechanism is far
   less sensitive than a person is.
5. **The one behavioural difference that showed up on the bench sat inside the ambiguity band.**
   In the still-felt arm of the diagnosis, both arms' choices were "too close to call", so the
   difference such left-over feeling makes there is settled by the seed, not by the history.

#### 4. What was assumed

- **The backstory is not run.** Each arm's people have only the history that arm gives them, so
  the independent variable is the only difference. This makes the bench clean and makes it
  unlike the shipped scenario, where everybody starts with three days behind them.
- **The lag is long enough that nothing is still felt** (forty fades, from the diagnosis
  measurement). Without that, the arms differ through left-over mood, which is a real effect
  but a short-lived one and not longitudinal. Separating those two was necessary and is the
  reason the first version of the diagnosis assertion failed.
- **The subject is answerable for what is missing**, so that two readings of the later event are
  live at once. Without that the competing reading scores zero and no amount of history can flip
  anything. This is a strong assumption and section 5 treats it as the main overfitting risk.
- **Twelve earlier events on twelve separate days** is not a claim about people. It is the
  smallest number above the flip the sweep found.
- **The morning arm writes the history into the backstory**, which changes what everybody else
  experiences too, not only the subject. It is an honest counterfactual but a broad one.

#### 5. What could be overfitting

Stated plainly, because it is the part I would attack if someone else wrote this.

- **The subject was chosen for the boundary.** Elena reads a search as `disrespect` at 0.610
  before any history, against a competing `threat` at 0.900. Mara, on the same event, reads
  disrespect at 1.04 and would never flip however much history she had, because her reading
  already wins. The mechanism only does anything for a person whose two readings start close.
  I did not pick her trait values, but I did pick her, and a different subject would have shown
  no effect at all.
- **The competing reading is supplied by guilt**, which is a different past event. The flip is
  really a contest between two histories, not between history and nothing. That is arguably
  more interesting, but it means the clean claim "history versus no history" is not quite what
  was measured.
- **The later event is the same kind of event as the history.** He goes through her things, and
  later he goes through her things. A wider class was tested (section 3, item 3) and only this
  one moved, so the mechanism may be narrower than "history changes how you read people".

#### 6. What was not tested

- Any personality configuration other than the shipped four. The window's width as a function of
  traits and values is unmeasured.
- Whether the effect appears for a subject whose competing reading comes from something other
  than guilt.
- Speech. Nobody speaks in this slice, so the `support` route that lowers `does_not_respect_me`
  was never exercised in anger, and no reading can be answered by anybody saying anything.
- Whether a flipped reading would change the act in a scenario with more to do. S1.6 already
  found people standing still most of a silent morning because nothing is worth doing; a want
  with no distinctive outlet cannot show.
- The `B` disposition model and the S1.6 fixes. Everything here ran on the shipped rules (`A`,
  `means: want`, untimed scripted events).

#### 6b. The predictions, checked

Committed in `held-out-predictions.md` before any S1.7 code existed (`1d59e43`).

| | Prediction | Result |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | T perceives it, keeps it as `disrespect`, both tendency beliefs rise; U's belief does not move | **held** (T 0.784 and 0.914; U 0.000 and 0.000) |
| P2 | With little history the meaning is unchanged and every downstream number is identical to 1e-9 while the weight differs | **held** (one earlier time against six: weight 0.653 against 0.819, downstream identical) |
| P3 | A history strength exists at which the meaning flips, and everything downstream changes together | **held** (at eleven; emotions, wants and the ranking all move at once) |
| P4 | `supplies_short` moves its want continuously, with no flat band | **held** (0.575 to 0.935 across nine steps) |
| P5 | Arm P matches the control, not the treatment | **held** (threat 0.900, identical to C) |
| P6 | The effect is identical at every lag, because no belief decays | **held** (one day to thirty days, identical) |
| P7 | `observe` is weight-sensitive but cannot flip; `search` can flip; the `demand` cannot learn | **MISSED on the third part.** The first two held. The demand *does* learn, through `corrected_by_a_junior_with_people_watching` reading `does_not_respect_me`, and is read from 1.445 to 1.811. I had only looked at `being_ordered_about`, whose belief nothing writes. Nothing followed anyway, but for a different reason than predicted: `disrespect` was already winning. |

Six held, one missed outright, one missed in a third of itself. Both misses are in the same
direction: I underestimated how often history moves an interpretation *weight*, and
overestimated how reliably a weight change stays invisible. The second miss matters, because a
single earlier event changing one morning in ten is the only sign in this slice that the
categorical account is not the whole story, and it is not explained. It is most likely the
left-over-feeling channel rather than the belief one, but that was not isolated.

#### 8. Recommendation

**MODIFY**, and the modification is not in this slice's subject matter.

| Component | Decision |
|---|---|
| The S1.7 instrument (`S17Measures`, the arms, the response curve) | **KEEP.** It measures where causal influence dies, which is the question worth asking of every later slice. |
| The belief loop as the longitudinal carrier | **KEEP.** It works, it is general, it is traceable, and it cost nothing. |
| Treating "the character remembers" as evidence of anything | **ABANDON.** This slice measured the gap directly: eleven memories that change nothing, and a twelfth that changes everything. |
| The categorical channel as the *only* route for history about people | **MODIFY**, next slice. See below. |
| Beliefs that never decay | **MODIFY**, and recognise it as the same defect as S1.6's never-fading memory. |
| The ledger | **REBUILD** when relationships matter. It is authored, mostly unread, and cannot support any claim. |

**The missing general mechanism, named as the brief requires.** Interpretation produces a
winning meaning *and a weight*, and throws the weight away. Everything downstream is therefore
blind to how clearly something was read. That is why history has to flip a category to matter at
all, and it is why a person who has been slighted eleven times feels exactly what a stranger
feels. The general fix is that **how strongly a reading won should reach how hard it lands** —
appraisal already takes an intensity scale (`reach × attention`), and the margin by which a
reading beat its alternatives is the obvious third factor. That is one multiplication, it is
general, it names no scenario, and it would convert the categorical channel into a graded one
without adding a modifier, a decay constant or a relationship meter.

It must not be done inside this slice, and I have not done it. It changes every number in every
existing trace, so it needs its own diagnosis, its own held-out predictions committed first, and
its own regression run. The test that would distinguish it from a patch is already built: the
response curve in section 2 should stop being flat.

**What the next experiment should test.** Whether making the margin of a reading reach its
intensity turns the step into a slope, and whether *that* reaches the act and survives a morning
above the noise. If it does not, the failure is in deliberation rather than in motivation, and
the honest conclusion will be that a silent house with seven actions cannot express a changed
inner life, which is a scenario problem and not an architecture one.

## diagnosis.md

### S1.7 diagnosis: what can carry a past event into a later decision

Written by hand from `diagnosis-before.md`, generated by `S17DiagnosisTests` on the shipped
rules with nothing changed. Everything below is measured by running the thing it describes,
not read off the source.

#### 1. Only one thing crosses midnight

Three things could carry an earlier event into a later decision, and they have completely
different lifetimes.

| Carrier | Lives for | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Memory** | the day it happened | `ScalerEval.Recall` filters `x.Day == ctx.Today`. A memory of an earlier day is kept forever and read by nothing that raises a want. |
| **Feeling** | about an hour | decays every fade; by sixteen fades nothing is left of six earlier events. |
| **Belief** | for ever | nothing anywhere decays one. Thirty days later it is exactly where it was. |

So a question about longitudinal causation in this architecture is, almost entirely, a
question about beliefs. The design brief's phrase "memory persistence" and its phrase "causal
relevance" turn out to name two different objects here, not two aspects of one.

#### 2. The ledger is nearly write-only

Eight kinds of ledger entry exist. The scenario data writes five of them, fourteen times in
all, every one as an authored `ledger_effects` block on a hand-written event. **No rule
anywhere turns what an event meant into a ledger entry**, so the record of what people have
done to each other is authored rather than simulated. Three of the eight kinds are read by any
rule at all; `searched_my_things`, written into Mara's ledger about Daniel at weight 0.8 by the
existing `p02`, is read by nothing.

This is why the accusation-and-compliance scenario the brief suggested would not have measured
the simulation: the variable it rests on has no wire attached at either end.

#### 3. Two kinds of belief, with different causal reach

A belief matters longitudinally only if experience can write it and something later reads it.
Both halves exist, and they come in two shapes.

| Belief | Written from experience by | Read by |
|---|---|---|
| `tendency(x, does_not_respect_me)` | any `disrespect` reading (up), any `support` reading (down) | interpretation only |
| `tendency(x, treats_me_like_a_child)` | any `disrespect` reading addressed to me | interpretation only |
| `tendency(x, needs_to_be_in_charge)` | **nothing** | interpretation only |
| `answerable_for(me, topic)` | doing the thing, witnessed | interpretation only |
| `supplies_short` | counting a short shelf, watching someone eat | interpretation **and the `guard_supplies` want** |
| `role_claim(me, leads_family)` | **nothing** | interpretation **and the `find_out` want** |

A belief a **motivation** rule reads moves a want directly and by any amount. A belief only
**interpretation** reads has to go through a reading first, and section 4 is what that costs.

#### 4. Nothing downstream of a reading can see history

Two independent facts, both measured:

- **No appraisal rule reads history.** All eighteen are weighed by traits, values and
  perceptiveness, and by nothing else. What an event feels like is decided by the reading it
  got, who you are, and who was watching.
- **A reading carries no strength with it.** `Simulation.Perceive` hands appraisal and the
  belief nudges the winning *meaning* and nothing else. The weight that reading won by is
  written to the trace and read by nothing.

Together these mean a belief about a person can reach a feeling **only by changing which
reading wins**. The measurement: the same person, the same later event, one earlier slight
against six. The belief differs (0.120 against 0.536), the reading is won by more (0.653
against 0.819), and every number after the reading is identical to nine decimal places, down
to the act chosen.

Run the same pair while the earlier events are still being felt and they do differ, but the
difference is in what the person is *carrying*, not in what this event stirred: the event's own
appraisal is identical in both, and only the left-over feeling from the earlier events moves.
That residue is gone within the hour. It is mood, not history.

#### 5. What this predicts, and what it rules out

The architecture should show **two channels with opposite characters**: a graded one, where a
belief a motivation rule reads moves behaviour a little at every step, and a categorical one,
where a belief only interpretation reads moves nothing at all until a reading flips and then
moves everything at once.

It also rules out the shape of a patch. Any mechanism that carried history as a hidden modifier
into behaviour would move behaviour smoothly with the modifier. If the response curve is flat
and then steps, what is being measured cannot be that.

#### 6. What the diagnosis does not settle

- Whether a reading can be made to flip at all by an amount of history a person could plausibly
  accumulate, and how much that is.
- Whether a changed reading, having changed the feelings and the wants, ever changes the act.
- Whether any of it survives a morning in which everybody else is also acting.

## diagnosis-before.md

### S1.7 diagnosis: what carries a past event into a later decision

Generated by `S17DiagnosisTests` on the shipped rules, with nothing changed. Measured rather than read off the code: each section runs the thing it describes.

#### 1. What survives a day boundary

One person is shown one event on day 1 that they read as a slight. Then days pass with nothing happening. At each later day their wants are raised exactly as a morning would raise them, and the three things that could carry the event are read off: the memory, the belief it moved, and the feeling it stirred.

| Day the wants are raised | Memory of it recalled (`find_out` memory term) | Belief `treats_me_like_a_child` | Strongest feeling left |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (the same day) | 0.000 | 0.120 | hurt 0.565 |
| 2 | 0.000 | 0.120 | hurt 0.445 |
| 3 | 0.000 | 0.120 | hurt 0.350 |
| 4 | 0.000 | 0.120 | hurt 0.276 |

A memory scaler only ever looks at the day being lived (`ScalerEval.Recall` filters on `x.Day == ctx.Today`), so a memory of an earlier day is kept and is never read by anything that raises a want. Feelings fade. **A belief is the only thing that crosses midnight with its strength intact**, and nothing anywhere decays one.

#### 2. The ledger is almost entirely write-only

| Ledger entry | Written by the scenario data | Read by any rule |
|---|---|---|
| `comforted_me` | 1 times | yes |
| `overruled_me` | 1 times | yes |

| `protected_me` | 1 times | yes |
| `proved_right` | 3 times | **no** |

| `raised_voice_at_me` | 1 times | **no** |
| `searched_my_things` | 1 times | **no** |

Every one of those writes is an authored `ledger_effects` block on a hand-written event. **No rule anywhere produces a ledger entry from what an event meant**, so the record of what somebody did to somebody else is authored rather than simulated. 3 of 8 entry kinds are read by anything at all.

#### 3. Which beliefs are learnable, and what reads them back

A belief matters longitudinally only if something can write it from experience and something else reads it later. The two columns are what make a loop.

Two shapes, and they behave nothing like each other. A belief read by a **motivation** rule moves a want directly, by any amount, at once. A belief read only by **interpretation** rules can only change what a later event comes to mean, and section 4 shows what that costs.

## held-out-predictions.md

### S1.7 hypothesis, the design, and held-out predictions

Written after inspecting the S1.6 code and rule data, and **before any S1.7 code exists**.
Nothing below has been run.

#### 0. Why not the scenario that was suggested

The brief proposed: Daniel accuses Mara, Elena defends her, later Daniel asks Mara to do
something, and we compare whether she complies. After reading the architecture I think that
scenario would mostly measure things that are not there, and would hide the one thing that is.
Four reasons, each checkable in the code rather than a matter of taste:

1. **There is no compliance.** The decision layer has seven actions: wait, observe, go_to,
   check_pantry, search_room, comfort, eat. Nothing represents agreeing or refusing to do what
   somebody asked, and no motivation rule mentions a request. "Does Mara comply?" would require
   a new action, a new want and new proposals, all invented for this scenario, and the result
   would be a measurement of the thing I had just written rather than of the simulation.
2. **The ledger entry it would rely on is inert.** `p02` (Daniel empties Mara's bag while she
   stands there) already authors `searched_my_things` into Mara's ledger about Daniel at weight
   0.8. **No rule anywhere reads `searched_my_things`.** Five of the eight ledger entry types in
   the vocabulary are written by the scenario data and read by nothing. An experiment resting on
   that record would be measuring a variable with no wire attached.
3. **Nothing in the pipeline ever writes a ledger entry.** All fourteen ledger writes in the data
   are authored `ledger_effects` on hand-written events. Interpretation and appraisal never
   produce one. So "the relationship changed because of what happened" is, today, authored rather
   than simulated, and a scenario that authors the grudge and then reads it back would be the
   "hidden modifier to predetermined behaviour" the brief warns against.
4. **An accusation is not in the vocabulary.** The nearest general concepts are a `demand`
   addressed to me, or a `search_belongings` action aimed at me. Inventing an accusation act
   would again be scenario-specific content.

What the architecture *does* have is a genuine, general, already-shipped longitudinal loop that
no experiment has tested yet. That is what S1.7 should test.

#### 1. What the architecture actually supports (from code, not from guesswork)

**The only two carriers that cross a day boundary are beliefs and the ledger.** A memory scaler
recalls only today: `ScalerEval.Recall` filters `x.Day == ctx.Today`. The ledger never decays but
is authored-only and mostly unread. Emotions decay every fade and are gone within a morning. So
**yesterday reaches today through beliefs, and essentially nothing else.**

**Beliefs are formed by general rules from what an event meant.** `belief_nudges` fire on the
*meaning* a person arrived at, not on the event: a reading of `disrespect` raises
`tendency($actor, does_not_respect_me)` by 0.15 plus pride, and a reading of `disrespect`
addressed to me raises `tendency($actor, treats_me_like_a_child)` by 0.12; `support` lowers
`does_not_respect_me`. None of these names a character, an act or a scenario.

**Those beliefs are then read back by interpretation rules**, which is the loop that makes
history matter:

| Belief | Written by | Read by |
|---|---|---|
| `tendency(x, does_not_respect_me)` | any `disrespect` reading (up), any `support` reading (down) | `corrected_by_a_junior_with_people_watching` 0.4, `being_watched` 0.3 |
| `tendency(x, treats_me_like_a_child)` | any `disrespect` reading addressed to me | `your_things_gone_through` 0.4, `being_watched` 0.4 |
| `tendency(x, needs_to_be_in_charge)` | **nothing** | `being_ordered_about` 0.4 |
| `supplies_short` | counting a short shelf, watching someone eat | `somebody_helping_themselves` 0.35, **and the `guard_supplies` want 0.5** |
| `answerable_for(me, missing_can)` | doing the thing, witnessed | `the_search_is_for_what_you_did` 1.0 |
| `role_claim(me, leads_family)` | **nothing** (authored) | `being_told_to_let_it_go` 0.3, **and the `find_out` want 0.3** |

So there is a real loop: be treated badly, come to believe that of the person, read the next
ambiguous thing they do more darkly, feel differently, want differently, act differently. Every
link is a general rule already shipped. S1.7 needs **no new mechanism** to test it.

#### 2. The structural prediction that makes this worth doing

Two facts, both confirmed in code, make a sharp and falsifiable prediction.

- **Interpretation weight is discarded.** `Simulation.Perceive` passes only
  `interpretation.Meaning` into appraisal and into the belief nudges. The weight that reading won
  by is recorded in the trace and used by nothing.
- **No appraisal rule reads history.** All eighteen appraisal rules are scaled only by traits,
  values and perceptiveness. Zero belief, ledger or memory scalers.

Therefore a belief about a person can only change what somebody feels **by changing which
meaning wins**. A history that moves `disrespect` from 0.86 to 0.94 produces byte-identical
emotion, salience, motivation and action. This predicts that interpersonal history is
**causally inert except at an interpretation boundary**, where it is abruptly decisive.

Beliefs about the *world* behave completely differently, because `supplies_short` and
`role_claim` are read directly by motivation rules, where any change moves the want
proportionally. So the architecture should have **two channels with opposite characters**:

- **Graded channel** (belief read by a motivation rule): continuous, any change moves behaviour a
  little.
- **Categorical channel** (belief read only by interpretation rules): a step function, nothing
  moves until the reading flips, then everything moves at once.

**This is the hypothesis S1.7 tests.** It is a claim about where causal influence survives and
where it dies, which is what the brief asked for, and it separates "the character remembers"
from "the memory changes what they do" by measuring the gap between them.

#### 3. The experiment

**Subject held constant. Later event held constant. Only history varies.**

The probe is a `search_belongings` event aimed at the subject, topic `missing_can`, witnessed.
It is chosen because two interpretation rules compete on it, and both are carried by history:

- `your_things_gone_through` produces **disrespect**, carried by `treats_me_like_a_child`
  (learnable)
- `the_search_is_for_what_you_did` produces **threat**, carried by `answerable_for` (from the
  night)

and because the two readings lead to genuinely different feelings (shame, anger, hurt,
frustration against fear and anxiety) and so to different wants (`restore_standing` and
`avoid_exposure` against `keep_peace`). It is also an event the silent morning already produces
by itself when somebody searches a room its owner is standing in, so it is not a synthetic probe.

The history is prior `search_belongings` events by the same actor on earlier days: he has gone
through my things before. Each is read as disrespect by the existing rules and nudges the two
tendency beliefs. No new rule, no new act, no authored ledger entry, no character named anywhere.

**Arms.**

| Arm | What differs | What it controls for |
|---|---|---|
| **C** control | no prior event | the baseline |
| **T** treatment | prior event, subject witnesses it | the effect |
| **U** unperceived | prior event happens, subject is not a witness | knowledge locality: world truth must not leak |
| **P** other actor | prior event perceived, but the later search is by a different person | relational specificity: this must not be global grumpiness |

**Measurements, at every stage of the chain, for each arm:** whether the event was perceived,
whether an experience was kept and with what meaning and salience, the belief and its
justification trace, the later reading and every rule's weight in it, the emotions and their
intensities, every want raised and its urgency, the ranked options with their appeal and cost,
the chosen action, and whether the whole path reconstructs from the trace alone.

**E2, the response curve.** Sweep the number of prior slights from 0 upward and record every
stage. This is the measurement that distinguishes a mechanism from a patch: a patch would move
behaviour smoothly with the modifier, and the architecture predicts a flat band followed by a
step. Run the same sweep on `supplies_short` (the graded channel) as an internal control, to
prove the instrument can see a graded effect where one exists.

**E3, time.** The same history at lags of minutes, same day, one day, two days and three days.
Beliefs have no decay anywhere in the code, so the prediction is that the effect is identical at
every lag, which is itself a finding and the mirror of the never-fading memory S1.6 found.

**E4, class generalisation.** Probe with several later events (`search_belongings` aimed at me,
`observe` aimed at me, a `demand` addressed to me, a junior refusing in front of others) against
C and T, to find which classes of later situation are history-sensitive at all, rather than
fitting one request.

**E5, the morning.** Full ninety-minute mornings with and without the prior event, on the shipped
rules (A) and on the configuration S1.6 recommended (B with both fixes), compared against the
S1.1 seed-noise floor. This asks whether any of it reaches behaviour over a morning.

#### 4. Definitions

- **Independent variable:** the subject's prior history with one other person, varied only by
  whether earlier events occurred and whether the subject perceived them.
- **Dependent variables, in order:** experience kept; belief confidence; the winning meaning of
  the later event and its weight; emotions and intensities; wants and urgencies; option scores;
  chosen action.
- **Controls:** same subject, same profile, same later event object, same seed, same room, same
  people present, same hunger. The unperceived arm and the other-actor arm.
- **Confounds I can see:** (a) the prior events also produce emotions that may not have decayed
  by the probe, which would be a within-day effect rather than a longitudinal one, so the lag
  sweep must include lags long enough for emotion to be gone; (b) prior events add memories of
  today if placed on the same day, which recall can read, so the across-day arms are the clean
  ones; (c) the probe itself nudges beliefs, so measurements must be taken from the probe's own
  decision, not after it.
- **What counts as evidence:** a stage-by-stage trace in which every link is a real parent
  record, plus the counterfactual arms differing in the way the mechanism predicts and the
  locality arm showing no effect at all.
- **Failure conditions, stated in advance:** if the belief moves but no meaning ever flips at any
  history strength, the categorical channel is real but unreachable and the answer to the brief's
  question is "no, not for beliefs about people". If the meaning flips but emotion, motivation or
  action do not follow, the chain is broken at that stage and I will say where. If behaviour
  differs in the morning but the trace cannot reconstruct why, that is not evidence and will be
  reported as such.

#### 5. Predictions, committed before running

Design conditions, the existing cast, the shipped rules unless stated.

- **P1.** In arm T the prior event is perceived, kept as an experience whose meaning is
  `disrespect`, and raises both `treats_me_like_a_child` and `does_not_respect_me` above their
  arm-C values. In arm U the event happens and the belief does not move at all.
- **P2.** With one prior slight, the later event's winning meaning is the **same** in T and C, and
  every downstream number (emotions, wants, option scores, chosen action) is **identical to
  within 1e-9**, while the interpretation weight differs. Memory persists; nothing follows.
- **P3.** There exists a number of prior slights at which the winning meaning flips, and at that
  point emotions, wants and the ranked options all change together, discontinuously.
- **P4.** The `supplies_short` sweep moves the `guard_supplies` want continuously from the first
  step, with no flat band. The two channels behave differently on the same instrument.
- **P5.** In arm P (the later search by a different person) the reading, emotions and wants match
  arm C, not arm T. The effect is about a person, not a mood.
- **P6.** The effect is identical at every lag from one day to three days, because no belief
  decays.
- **P7.** Of the four later-event classes, `observe` aimed at me is history-sensitive in weight
  but can never flip, because only one rule fires on it; `search_belongings` can flip; the
  `demand` cannot learn, because `needs_to_be_in_charge` is written by nothing.
- **P8.** Over a full morning, with one prior slight the action profiles in treatment and control
  are identical on a shared seed.

**Held out:** the boundary-crossing history strength predicted in P3 is not chosen in advance; I
will report the number the sweep finds. `leo_ate_it` seeds 201 to 240 are reserved for any
morning measurement, and no test has run a seed above 180.

#### 6. What this slice will not do

No new action, no new want, no compliance, no relationship meter, no trauma, no decay constant,
no new emotion, no LLM, no Unity, no character-specific rule, and no change to any existing rule
or number. If the experiment shows a missing general mechanism, it will be named and specified,
and left for the next slice to implement against a fresh prediction.

Going through somebody's things is history-sensitive because two readings compete on it and history carries one of them, and which of the two wins is what decides how it feels. Being watched is read nearly two and a half times as darkly and nothing follows, because only one rule ever fires on it, so the extra weight has nothing to beat. A demand is read more darkly too, and nothing follows, because `disrespect` was already winning without any history. A refusal in front of others is read as support by this person either way; the slighted reading of it rises with history and stays second.

Being read *more strongly* is therefore not the same as being read *differently*, and only the second has any consequence. A later situation is sensitive to history exactly when history can change which reading wins, which needs two readings that are close and at least one of them carried by a belief that experience can write.

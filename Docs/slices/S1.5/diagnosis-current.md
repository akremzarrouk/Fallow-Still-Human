# S1.5 diagnosis: where wants come from

Generated by `S15DiagnosisTests`. Shipped rules, the five design conditions; decisions from seeds 1 to 10, behaviour from the S1 batch, seeds 1 to 40.

Each term of a want is sorted by what it read. **Circumstance:** the body, a memory of today, a live feeling. **History:** a belief with evidence behind it, a ledger entry. **Authored:** a belief written into the person with no evidence. **Disposition:** a trait, a value. A want is **FromCircumstance** when circumstance and history carry at least half of it, **MostlyDisposition** when they carry something but less than half, and **Unsupported** when nothing that happened is behind it at all.

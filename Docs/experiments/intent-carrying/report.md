# Intent-carrying action: does preserving meaning change the life?

Date: 2026-09-20. Status: complete, stopped for review. **Not S1.8.** One small causal
experiment. The only change to `Fallow.Core` is a hook that lets an act's event carry what the
person took themselves to be doing, and the event field it fills, which is null unless an
experiment sets it. No rule, number, want, action, appraisal or interpretation was changed, and
no want-to-meaning mapping was shipped.
Reproduce with `./run-tests.sh`. **Suite: PENDING.**

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `predictions.md` | Ten predictions, written before the experiment was run and kept as written |
| `results.md` | Generated by `IntentCarryingExperimentTests`: the act and the controls, the same search under three meanings, every search in fifty mornings, and what can read an intent at all |
| `intent-carrying-all.md` | All of the above merged |

**In short.** Yes, and only where something already listens.

- The meaning survives the boundary in **373 of 373** carried acts: the searcher reads their own
  search as their own intention instead of as `neutral`, and keeps it that way in memory.
- With `take_responsibility`, which no rule downstream reads, the effect **stops at memory**:
  nothing was felt, no want moved, no act changed, and all fifty mornings ran exactly as the
  baseline did.
- With `assert_authority`, which one appraisal rule reads, the same search became anger, and in
  the strongest case that ran all the way to a different act and a different morning: **61 of
  72 decisions differed**.
- That propagation went through **other people**. The anger showed, the room read it as somebody
  not holding together, and their distress came back to him.
- Under a plausible mapping over fifty mornings the effect is small: 6 of 373 searches stirred
  anything, **0** changed the act the searcher took next, and **1 of 50** mornings changed course.

So preserving intent is necessary but not sufficient. What decides whether it matters is not
the boundary, which now carries it, but whether anything downstream reads that kind of meaning.
Three of the six self-meanings have no reader at all, and **no motivation rule can read a memory
of any of them**.

---

## 1. Hypothesis

If a person's intentional reason for an act survives the act -> world-event -> perception
boundary, then their later interpretation of that event can differ from the baseline in which
the reason is discarded; and if it differs, it may propagate into later internal state and
later decisions.

Tested as: the same character, same world, same physical act, same target, same physical
outcome, same seed, same random stream, **only the carried meaning differs**.

## 2. Selected action

**Searching a room.** Chosen because it needs the least new theory:
- It is the one morning act the shipped data already carries an intent for: `p02` of the
  backstory is `search_belongings` with `intent: take_responsibility`.
- Two different wants already lead it in real mornings (`find_out` 467 times in 50 mornings,
  `restore_standing` 7), so the same visible act already serves two ends.
- It makes a world event with witnesses, so the social path is available too.
- It has no physical effect that could differ between arms: it sets a "searched" flag and
  finds nothing, because there is nothing in the world to find.

The two real searches used were found by running the baseline and taking the first search led
by each want, in condition and seed order:

| Case | Who | Condition, seed | Minutes | Led by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Daniel | `daniel_ate_it`, seed 1 | 4 to 12 | `find_out` 0.757 |
| B | Mara | `mara_ate_it`, seed 3 | 78 to 86 | `restore_standing` 0.271 |

## 3. Selected intentional distinction

`take_responsibility` against `assert_authority`. Both are in the shipped `self_meanings`
vocabulary. Neither was invented here.

**Why `take_responsibility` is defensible for a search:** the data already says it. `p02` is
the same action name, on the same topic, with that intent.

**Why `assert_authority` is defensible for a search:** the house already reads such a search
that way from the outside. The interpretation rule `authority_taken_without_asking` fires when
a parent sees a junior act on `missing_can` or `who_decides`, and gives `challenge`. In the
baseline of case A, **Elena reads Daniel's search as a challenge** while Daniel himself reads
it as nothing at all. The appraisal rule for `assert_authority` is keyed to the concern
`respect`, which is what `restore_standing` is built from.

**What was deliberately not done.** No `Want -> SelfMeaning` mapping was implemented in the
simulation. Which reason an act expresses is a claim about people, and this experiment is not
entitled to make it. In parts 1 and 2 the meaning is stipulated for one act by the harness. In
part 3 a mapping is stipulated by the harness to count how far a meaning reaches, and it is
labelled a stipulation in the generated document.

## 4. Implementation

Exactly two things changed in `Fallow.Core`, both in `Sim/SilentMorning.cs`:

| Change | What it does |
|---|---|
| `public Func<string, ActionOption, Decision, string> IntentOfAct { get; set; }` | A hook, null by default. Given the actor, the act and the decision that chose it, it returns the meaning the act expresses, or null |
| `Complete` computes `intent` once and passes it to `Happened` (and to `Walk`), which puts it on the `WorldEvent` in place of the `null` that was hard-coded there | the act's event carries it |

That is the whole plumbing: one file, 33 added lines against 12 replaced, of which the hook and
its comment are 13 and the rest are the same calls with one argument more. Nothing else in
`Fallow.Core`, and nothing in the data, was touched. **With the hook unset the event gets
`null`, which is exactly what every slice up to here ran**, and the suite and the fingerprint
prove it.

Everything downstream is the existing pipeline, unchanged:
- `Interpreter.Interpret` already returns the intent to the actor ("knew their own intention")
  when an event carries one. It was written for authored history.
- `Appraiser` already keys on the meaning, whatever its source.
- The experience already keeps the meaning, and salience is already built from what was stirred.

The experiment itself is `Tests/EditMode/IntentCarryingExperimentTests.cs` (new, 4 tests).

## 5. Controls

| Control | How it was ensured | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Repeat | the baseline run twice, same seed | identical, act for act (72 acts) |
| 2. Same act, different intent | the same morning, the same act, three arms | the core experiment |
| 3. Intent removed | (a) the hook set and returning nothing; (b) the same hook shape carrying `null` on the same act | identical to the baseline, both ways, and in 50 of 50 mornings in part 3 |
| 4. Randomness | the random stream is forked per person and per minute from the seed (`Rng.Fork(person@minute)`), so an arm cannot shift it. Checked: every decision in both arms falls at the same minute for the same person | held |
| 5. Physical outcome | the house at the actor's next decision: portions, and everybody's room and hunger | identical: `portions 2; daniel in kitchen hunger 0.605, elena in kitchen 0.641, leo in kitchen 0.498, mara in kitchen 0.546` |
| 5b. The act's own event | every field but the intent: action, topic, tone, valence, target, summary, minute | identical |
| 6. Act identification | the act is identified by the minute it finishes, which is the minute it becomes an event, and the arms are identical until then | exact |

An earlier version of the harness identified the act as "the nth search by this person",
counting decisions. That is wrong, because an interrupted or carried-on search makes two
decisions and one event, so the counts disagree; it attached the intent to nothing in case B.
The failure was caught by an assertion, not by inspection, and the harness was fixed to use the
finishing minute.

## 7. Causal propagation

```
intent                assert_authority, carried on the event the search became
   |                  PROPAGATED
event                 WorldEvent.Intent, the field that was null for every morning act
   |                  PROPAGATED
perception            the actor, and only the actor: Interpreter returns the intent
   |                  PROPAGATED (373 of 373)
interpretation        "knew their own intention", weight 1.00, no runner-up
   |                  PROPAGATED, and REPLACED what was there: the reading "neutral" is gone
appraisal             asserting_yourself_is_done_angry -> anger 0.61 (respect)
   |                  PROPAGATED for assert_authority; STOPPED for take_responsibility (no rule reads it)
emotion               anger, live, and above the threshold at which feelings show
   |                  PROPAGATED
memory / belief       the experience keeps the meaning and a higher salience; no belief moved
   |                  memory PROPAGATED; belief STOPPED (no nudge reads a self-meaning)
motivation            restore_standing 0.190 -> 0.485; and, through the social path below,
   |                  keep_peace, avoid_exposure and both look_after wants
later decision        go_to->back_room -> observe:elena, and 61 of 72 decisions after it
```

**The social path, which was not predicted.** In case A the largest part of the effect did not
stay inside Daniel:

1. His anger (0.61) became his strongest feeling, and anger is one of the feelings that show.
2. `ShowWhatShows` made a `show_distress` event about him, in the room.
3. Elena and Mara read it as **concern**, which made them anxious.
4. Their distress then showed, in the same minute.
5. Daniel read **their** distress as concern, and his own anxiety went 0.186 -> 0.803 and his
   fear 0.178 -> 0.737.
6. Those feelings are what his other wants read, which is why four wants moved, not one.

Every step is existing machinery, none of it written or touched for this experiment.

**Where it stops:**
- **At the witnesses' reading of the act.** No interpretation rule can ask about an intent, so
  nobody reads the search itself differently. They only see what it did to him afterwards.
- **At belief.** No belief nudge reads a self-meaning; 0 beliefs moved in 373 carried acts.
- **At the memory, for later wants.** No motivation rule reads a memory carrying a
  self-meaning: every memory term in `decisions.json` names `concern`, `threat` or
  `reassurance`. A preserved intent can only reach a later decision through what it stirred at
  the time.
- **At the trace link.** The event's record still rests on nothing, so even with the meaning
  preserved, no memory of the act can be walked back to the decision that chose it. The
  *content* of the reason now survives; the *causal link* still does not.

## 8. Quantitative results

Fifty mornings (five design conditions, seeds 1 to 10), with every search carrying the meaning
of the want that led it (`find_out` -> `take_responsibility`, `restore_standing` ->
`assert_authority`). The mapping is a stipulation of the harness, not a rule.

| | Count |
|---|---|
| Searches carrying an intent | 373 (`take_responsibility` 367, `assert_authority` 6) |
| ... the actor recovered it as their own intention | **373 of 373** |
| ... what the actor kept of the act changed | **373 of 373** |
| ... the memory's salience changed | 6 (the `assert_authority` ones) |
| ... the actor's feelings at their next decision changed | 6 |
| ... the actor's wants at their next decision changed | 6 |
| ... the act the actor chose next changed | **0** |
| ... the actor's beliefs changed by the end of the morning | **0** |
| ... any witness read the search differently | **0** |
| Mornings whose whole course changed | **1 of 50** |
| Mornings identical with the intent removed again | 50 of 50 |

The sample of `assert_authority` acts is 6, which is too few for any rate. What it shows is
existence, not frequency: 6 of 6 stirred something, 0 of 6 changed the very next act, and one
of them changed the morning two decisions later, through somebody else.

In the forced arms of part 2, where the meaning is put on a search that a different want led,
the effect is much larger (61 of 72 decisions in case A). That is a counterfactual, not a
frequency: it says what the architecture can carry, not how often it would.

**All ten predictions held** (`predictions.md`): P1 and P2 (survival), P3 (no reader, no
effect), P4 and P5 (anger, and `restore_standing` rising), P6 (the act changes in under a fifth
of mornings: 1 of 50), P7 (no witness changed), P8 (identical physical outcome), P9 (removal
restores the baseline), P10 (salience rose only where something was stirred).

## 9. Negative results

- **`take_responsibility` changed nothing but the memory.** 367 acts, 367 changed memories, 0
  changed feelings, wants, acts or mornings. Carrying a meaning that nothing reads is inert.
- **No witness ever saw the act differently.** The reason is private by construction.
- **No belief moved**, in any arm.
- **The actor's next act never changed under the stipulated mapping** (0 of 373).
- **Three of the six self-meanings have no appraisal rule**, and none has a belief nudge or a
  motivation rule that reads a memory of it.
- **The reading is replaced, not added to.** With an intent carried, the actor keeps only "what
  I meant"; the reading they would have had of their own act ("neutral", or whatever the rules
  would have made of it) is gone. An experience holds one meaning.
- **The trace still does not link the act's event to the decision.**

## 10. Information-loss map: what is still discarded

| Boundary | Before this experiment | Now |
|---|---|---|
| act -> world event | the reason: gone | **carried**, when a hook supplies it |
| world event -> the actor's perception | read as a witness would | **the actor knows their own intention** |
| the actor's other reading of their own act | - | **newly lost**: the intent replaces it |
| world event -> a witness's perception | no reason | unchanged: no rule can read an intent |
| perception -> appraisal | meaning only | unchanged, and only 3 of 6 self-meanings have a reader |
| perception -> belief | meaning only | unchanged: no nudge reads a self-meaning |
| memory -> later motivation | meaning only, and only `concern`, `threat`, `reassurance` | unchanged: **a remembered intent can never press a want** |
| act's event -> the decision, in the trace | unlinked | unchanged: still unlinked |
| how strongly it was wanted | discarded at the choice | unchanged |

## 11. Classification

| Claim | Label |
|---|---|
| A reason can be carried across the act -> event -> perception boundary with the existing architecture | **PROVEN** (373 of 373; two lines of plumbing) |
| The actor's interpretation of their own act changes when it is carried | **PROVEN** (373 of 373, from "neutral" to the carried meaning, at weight 1.00) |
| The memory changes with it | **PROVEN** (373 of 373) |
| A carried meaning can change appraisal, emotion, motivation and the act chosen | **PROVEN** for `assert_authority` (case A: anger 0.61, four wants moved, the act changed, 61 of 72 decisions differed) |
| A carried meaning does so only where a rule already reads that meaning | **PROVEN** on this pair (`take_responsibility`: 367 acts, nothing stirred) |
| The main route from a private meaning to behaviour is **social**, through the feeling showing | **PLAUSIBLE**, strongly supported by case A, seen in 2 cases of 2 where anything moved at all |
| Under a plausible mapping the effect on behaviour is small | **PLAUSIBLE** (1 morning of 50; 0 immediate act changes of 373), on a sample of 6 acts that could stir anything |
| Preserving intent alone is sufficient to make the life different | **FAILED** |
| The pairing of a want to a meaning | **UNPROVEN** and not attempted: no evidence here says which meaning a want expresses |
| Whether intent should be inferable by witnesses | **UNKNOWN**: nothing in the architecture can do it, and nothing here tested it |

**Against the brief's outcomes:** the result is **A and C at once**. Strong support where a
reader exists (RESULT A), weak support where none does (RESULT C). It is not B (the difference
is not discarded downstream; it propagates when it exists), not D, and not E: the architecture
represented the distinction cleanly, with no redesign.

## 12. Decision

**KEEP.**

- The direction is validated: preserving intentional meaning across the action boundary is
  cheap, fits the architecture as it stands, is inert until asked for, and can reach behaviour.
- Keep the hook, unset. It changes nothing while no experiment uses it, and the suite proves it.
- Do **not** ship a want-to-meaning mapping on this evidence. Nothing here says which meaning a
  want expresses, and the whole effect depends on that choice.
- Do not add readers for the three self-meanings that have none. That is the next question, not
  a patch.

## 13. Next experiment

The smallest experiment the evidence justifies: **separate the private effect from the social
one.**

Case A's large effect went through other people seeing the anger. Case B's, with nobody in the
room, changed the wants and not the act. So the open question is whether preserving a reason
matters to the person who holds it, or only when it leaks into the room:

- the same act, the same carried meaning, with and without an audience;
- measure how much of the downstream difference survives when nobody is there to see the
  feeling;
- no new rules, no mapping, the existing harness.

If the private effect is near zero, then what a preserved intent buys is a **social** signal,
and the reason it cannot do more is the one this experiment found: no want can read a memory of
what you meant.

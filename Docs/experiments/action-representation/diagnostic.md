# Action representation: diagnostic

Generated by `ActionRepresentationAuditTests`, a diagnostic-only, read-only test. It runs the five design conditions, seeds 1-10, under the primary baseline (and Model A for comparison where stated), and looks at what each morning produced: every decision through the decision hook, every world event the morning created, what each mind kept of each event, and the trace. It changes nothing in `Fallow.Core`, the data, or any run.

An act is linked to the world event it produced by its actor and time: the events a person authors after a decision and before their next one are that act's (a walk makes up to two, leaving and arriving, and only if somebody is there; standing still makes none; an act stopped part way makes none). An act that was stopped, thought over and carried on has two decisions and one event, which is counted with the later decision.

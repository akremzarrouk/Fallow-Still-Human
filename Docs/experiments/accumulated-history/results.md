# Accumulated history: results against the predictions

The measurements are in `measurements.md`, generated by `AccumulatedHistoryExperimentTests`.
This is the assessment against `predictions.md`, which was written before the experiment ran.

## Runs

| | |
|---|---|
| Subjects | Elena (S1.7's) and Mara (the episode experiment's), with Daniel as the other |
| Earlier times (N) | 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 16 |
| Later events | he goes through her things again; he watches her |
| Also varied | whether she believes she is answerable for the missing can |
| Probe arms | 80 (2 subjects x 2 events x 2 answerable x 10 N) |
| Live mornings | 2 subjects x 10 seeds x 3 history levels = 60, in the condition where each subject is answerable (`elena_fed_mara`, `mara_ate_it`) |
| Randomness | the probe arms use a fixed stream; the live mornings are paired by seed, and the history is in the backstory, so it cannot shift the morning's stream |
| New code or rules | **none**. S1.7's harness, which is ordinary scenario data. Nothing in `Fallow.Core` or the data was changed |

## Predictions

| | Prediction | Result | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | No history means no relevant accumulated history | **PASS for Elena, FAILED for Mara** | Elena at N=0: both beliefs 0.000. Mara at N=0: `treats_me_like_a_child` **0.550, seeded by her character file**. Her "no history" condition is not a blank relationship |
| P2 | One earlier time produces belief through existing mechanisms | **PASS** | Elena N=1: `treats_me_like_a_child` 0.120, `does_not_respect_me` 0.185, from the two shipped nudges |
| P3 | It accumulates, each step adding less, never reaching 1 | **PASS** | 0.120, 0.226, 0.319, 0.400, 0.536, 0.640, 0.721, 0.784, 0.871 across N=1..16 |
| P4 | The later event is perceived identically in every arm | **PASS** | same event, same access; in the live mornings Elena's circumstances and her whole morning up to the probe were identical in 10 of 10 |
| P5 | The winning **label** differs between two history levels | **PASS** | Elena, being searched while answerable: `threat` up to N=10, **`disrespect` from N=12**. In live mornings, 10 of 10 seeds |
| P6 | Where the label does not change, the weight rises with N | **PASS** | Mara: 1.260, 1.282, 1.301, 1.317, 1.332, 1.356, 1.375, 1.390, 1.401, 1.417 |
| P7 | Where the label changes, appraisal, emotion and wants change with it | **PASS** | anxiety 0.688 + fear 0.555 become hurt 0.565, frustration 0.402, anger 0.313, shame 0.161; `find_out` 0.635 -> 0.234, `keep_peace` 0.439 -> gone, `restore_standing` 0 -> 0.233 |
| P8 | The act chosen usually does not change | **PASS** | the probe arms: `search_room` in all 80. The live mornings: the act was the same in 10 of 10, and only the **reason** differed (`find_out` -> `get_food`) |
| P9 | Later behavioural differences are mostly cascade and not attributable | **PASS** | Mara's live mornings diverged before the probe in 10 of 10 (the history is in her backstory), so her later differences are cascade and were excluded from the counts |
| P10 | Removing the history restores the no-history result | **PASS** | three controls: run twice, identical; history she never saw, identical to N=0 downstream; history taken away, identical |
| Subject | Elena flips near N=12 | **PASS** | between N=10 (disrespect 0.890 against threat 0.900) and N=12 (0.919 against 0.900) |
| Subject | Mara never flips | **PASS** | `disrespect` at every N, because it starts 0.360 ahead |
| Subject | Being watched never flips for anybody | **PASS** | one rule reads that event, so there is nothing to overtake |

## Where the causal chain stops

For Elena the chain runs: earlier events -> perceived -> read as disrespect -> two belief nudges
-> belief accumulates -> later identical event -> **different label** -> different appraisal ->
different emotions -> different wants -> **the same act**.

It stops at the act, and the reason is the one the Action Representation Audit named: the wants
that the two readings produce (`find_out` against `restore_standing` and `get_food`) ask for the
same acts. In the live morning her act is `check_pantry` either way; all that changes is which
want is credited with it.

For Mara the chain stops one stage earlier, at the label: the weight of her reading rises from
1.260 to 1.417 and nothing downstream moves at all, because weight is discarded once the
argmax has chosen.

# One episode: results against the predictions

The measurements are in `measurements.md`, generated by `EpisodeExperimentTests`. This is the
assessment against the predictions committed in `predictions.md` before the experiment ran.

## Runs

| | |
|---|---|
| Conditions of the house | `daniel_ate_it` (Mara took nothing) and `mara_ate_it` (Mara is the one who took the can) |
| Seeds | 1 to 10 each; 9 and 8 usable (a seed is used only if Daniel and Mara are in the same room at both moments) |
| Arms per seed | 3: **A** she saw it, **C** the same thing happened and she did not perceive it, **B** it never happened |
| Mornings run | 9x3 + 8x3 + the seed-finding runs and the six control mornings |
| Random streams | identical: forked per person and minute from the seed, so an arm cannot shift them |
| The intrusion | minute 8 in every seed |
| The later watching | minute 67 (`daniel_ate_it`) and 57 (`mara_ate_it`): the first minute after 38 at which the two of them were in the same room |
| New code or rules in `Fallow.Core` | **none**. No rule, number, want, action or mapping was added or changed. The two events are put in through `SilentMorning.Happen`, which already existed for experiments |

## First divergence

In every seed the first difference is Mara's own next decision, at minute 9, one minute after
the intrusion. Under `daniel_ate_it` seed 1 she searches the room for `restore_standing` where
in C she would have sat with Elena three minutes later.

## Later differences

Her decisions differ from C in between 1 and 30 of her ~20 to 32 decisions, and the last
difference falls at a median of minute 66 (`daniel_ate_it`) and minute 89 (`mara_ate_it`). After
the first divergence these are cascade: once she acts differently, everything downstream may
differ, and nothing in the machine attributes those later differences to the memory.

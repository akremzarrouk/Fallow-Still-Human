## report.md

### One episode: can something happen to a person and change what they do?

Date: 2026-09-20. Status: complete, stopped for review. **Not a system audit and not S1.8.**
Nothing in `Fallow.Core` and nothing in the data was changed: no rule, number, want, action,
meaning or mapping. Two events are put into a live morning through `SilentMorning.Happen`,
which already existed for experiments, and everything between and after them is the simulation.
Reproduce with `./run-tests.sh`. **Suite: PENDING.**

| File | What it is |

**In short. Yes, for about half an hour.**

Daniel goes through Mara's bag while she is standing there. She does not experience that as a
search: she reads it as **disrespect**, it hurts (0.70), and it shames and angers her. It stops
what she was doing. A minute later she does something she would not otherwise have done. Her
wants are different in **17 of 17** runs, her act in **7 of 17** immediately.

Then it fades. An hour later Daniel stands watching her, and she reads that exactly as she
would have read it anyway: her **reading never differed, in 17 of 17 runs**. What the intrusion
left behind permanently is a belief that her brother treats her like a child, and that belief
enters the later reading only as **weight**, which the deliberator discards.

So the machine can carry a lived moment. It cannot yet carry a life: the only channel that
reaches behaviour is feeling, and feeling decays within the hour; the only channel that lasts
is belief, and belief reaches later behaviour through a number that nothing reads.

---

#### 1. Hypothesis

That the mechanisms already proved separately would compose: something happens to a person,
they perceive and interpret it, it changes their state, the state persists, and later they
behave differently because of it, with no rule naming the memory and nothing scripting the
later behaviour.

#### 2. Episode

In a live morning of the shipped scenario, with the whole cast running normally:

1. **Minute 8 (SETUP).** Daniel empties Mara's bag out while she is standing there.
   `search_belongings`, topic `missing_can`, target Mara, tone neutral, no intent, no authored
   ledger or belief effects. It is the same event shape the simulation makes for itself when
   somebody searches a room, and it reaches people by the route their own acts reach each other.
2. **Everything between (the simulation).** Nobody is told to do anything.
3. **Minute 67 or 57 (SETUP).** Daniel watches her across the room. `observe`, target Mara. The
   minute is the first after 38 at which the two of them are in the same room, found in an
   untouched morning.
4. **To the end of the morning (the simulation).**

Two conditions of the house were used, because Mara's own guilt is part of what an intrusion
means: `daniel_ate_it`, where she took nothing, and `mara_ate_it`, where she is the one who
took the can.

#### 3. Conditions

| | |
|---|---|
| **A** | She was there and saw it |
| **C** | The same thing happened, in the same room, at the same minute, and she did not perceive it |
| **B** | It never happened |

Same content, same seed, same random streams (forked per person and minute, so an arm cannot
shift them). **A against C** is the experiment: identical world, identical events, only her
perception of one of them differs. **B** is the reference for what the house does when nothing
happens at all.

#### 5. Divergence point

**Her own next decision, one minute after the intrusion, in every seed.** The chain to it is:
perception (she was there) -> interpretation (`your_things_gone_through`, addressed to her, so
disrespect) -> appraisal (five rules) -> emotion (hurt, frustration, shame, anger) -> the
interruption threshold (it landed at 0.70, above 0.45, so it stopped her) -> motivation
(`avoid_exposure` reads shame and fear, `restore_standing` reads shame and anger) ->
deliberation -> act.

#### 6. Downstream consequence

- **Immediately:** her wants differ in 17 of 17 runs, and what she does differs in 7 of 17. In
  the other 10 she does the same thing at the same minute, feeling quite differently: the
  Decision Sensitivity finding, in a lived case.
- **Over the morning:** her decisions differ from C in 1 to 30 of her 20 to 32 decisions, the
  last at a median of minute 66 and 89. After the first divergence these are cascade, and
  nothing in the machine attributes them to the memory.
- **An hour later:** her reading of the later encounter never differs. Her feelings and wants
  differ at that point in 9 of 17, but by then that is the cascade of a different morning, not
  the intrusion still being felt: in `daniel_ate_it`, where the gap is 59 minutes, her feelings
  at minute 66 are down to gratitude and relief in both arms.

#### 10. What was actually demonstrated

- **An episode composes.** An event a person actually perceived, interpreted, felt, remembered
  and was changed by, produced a different act one minute later, through six existing stages and
  with nothing scripting the outcome. PROVEN for the immediate window.
- **It is her experience, not the world, that does it.** In C the same event happened in the
  same room at the same minute and she did not perceive it: nothing about her changed. Her
  morning still differed, because the others saw it and behaved differently around her, which is
  itself the social pipeline working.
- **The reading is the human part.** She does not respond to "a search". She responds to
  disrespect, and only because it was addressed to her and she is proud and values her autonomy.
  Leo in the same position would not read it the same way.
- **The effect on wants is total and the effect on acts is partial.** 17 of 17 against 7 of 17.

#### 11. What was not demonstrated

- **That the episode lasts.** An hour later her reading of a second act by the same person is
  unchanged in 17 of 17 runs. The feeling is gone and the belief is inert at the argmax.
- **That being wronged changes how she treats him.** She sits with Daniel afterwards as often as
  in the arms where he never touched her things. Nothing connects the two.
- **That the room takes care of her because of it.** Her distress was already showing before the
  intrusion, and she was sat with slightly *less* often in A than in C.
- **That the later differences are the memory.** After the first changed act, the cascade is
  real but unattributable.
- **That any of this generalises past one pair of events**, one injured party, and 17 mornings.

#### 12. Classification

| Claim | Label |
|---|---|
| A perceived event can change a later act through existing mechanisms, with no rule naming the memory | **PROVEN** (17 of 17 wants, 7 of 17 acts, first divergence one minute later) |
| The difference is her perception, not the world's state | **PROVEN** (arm C: identical event, no access, no change in her) |
| The interpretation is person-specific and is what makes it an episode rather than an incident | **PROVEN** on this case (disrespect, from her pride and her autonomy) |
| The influence on behaviour decays with the feeling | **PROVEN**: at a gap of 59 minutes, her reading, feelings and act are those of a morning in which it never happened |
| The durable trace (belief) reaches later behaviour | **FAILED** on this episode: it changes the weight of a later reading, which is discarded |
| Being wronged by somebody changes how she acts toward that person | **FAILED**: no channel exists |
| The later cascade is caused by the memory | **UNPROVEN**, and unattributable in the current trace |
| Repetition would flip the later reading, as S1.7 showed for twelve slights | **PLAUSIBLE**, untested here |

#### 13. Decision

**KEEP.**

The episode is real, and the composition works: perception, interpretation, appraisal, emotion,
memory, belief, motivation, deliberation and action ran in sequence on a live morning and
produced a different life, with nothing scripted past the two events. Keep the harness, keep the
episode as a regression case, and keep the direction.

What the experiment found is not a fault in the composition but a **limit on its duration**: the
machine carries a moment, not a history.

#### 14. Next step

The smallest experiment the evidence justifies: **measure the half-life of a lived moment.**

Run this same episode with the second encounter at a range of gaps (5, 10, 20, 40, 60 minutes)
and find where the difference disappears at each stage: feelings, wants, act, reading. It needs
no new code, no new rules and no new events, and it turns today's single observation ("it is
gone by an hour") into the curve that says what a durable episode would have to beat.

Only after that is it worth asking the real question this experiment raises, which is a design
question and not a measurement: **what, other than a feeling, should make a person act on
something that happened to them an hour ago?** Today the answer in the machine is nothing: a
belief can change what a later event means only by a weight that the argmax throws away.

## predictions.md

### First end-to-end human episode: predictions

Written **before the experiment was run**, and kept as written.

The episode: in a live morning, Daniel goes through Mara's things while she is standing there
(injected, **SETUP**). Half an hour later he watches her across the room (injected, **SETUP**).
Everything between and after is whatever the simulation does.

Three conditions, same seed and same stream: **A** she was there and saw it; **C** the same
thing happened and she did not perceive it; **B** it never happened.

| # | Prediction | Falsified if |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | In A she perceives the intrusion and keeps it; in C she keeps nothing of it | she keeps nothing in A, or something in C |
| P2 | In A she reads it as **disrespect** and it stirs hurt, frustration, anger and shame, by the existing rules | she reads it as something else, or nothing is stirred |
| P3 | Her beliefs about Daniel move in A and not in C: `treats_me_like_a_child` and `does_not_respect_me` | neither moves |
| P4 | At her next decision after it, A differs from C in what she wants (`avoid_exposure` and `restore_standing` higher) | her wants are identical |
| P5 | Her hurt is strong enough to show, so the room learns she is upset, and somebody sits with her more often in A than in C | it never shows, or being sat with is no more likely |
| P6 | At the later encounter, her **reading** is the same in A and C (`disrespect` either way): the belief the first event moved changes the weight, and weight is discarded | the meaning itself differs |
| P7 | At the later encounter her **feelings and wants** differ between A and C, from the residue of the first event rather than from what it meant | they are identical |
| P8 | Her act differs from C at some point in most seeds, without any rule naming the memory | her whole morning is identical in most seeds |
| P9 | B (never happened) and C (did not see it) leave Mara herself in the same state; any difference between them comes from other people | Mara differs between B and C |
| P10 | With nothing injected the morning is exactly the shipped baseline | it is not |

**The strongest result available** would be P1 to P5 and P8 together: she is intruded on, it
hurts, the hurt shows, somebody comes to her, and she does something she would not otherwise
have done. **The most likely negative** is P6 holding and P7 failing, which would mean the
episode carries forward only as long as the feeling lasts, and nothing of it survives as
meaning.

#### First divergence

In every seed the first difference is Mara's own next decision, at minute 9, one minute after
the intrusion. Under `daniel_ate_it` seed 1 she searches the room for `restore_standing` where
in C she would have sat with Elena three minutes later.

#### Later differences

Her decisions differ from C in between 1 and 30 of her ~20 to 32 decisions, and the last
difference falls at a median of minute 66 (`daniel_ate_it`) and minute 89 (`mara_ate_it`). After
the first divergence these are cascade: once she acts differently, everything downstream may
differ, and nothing in the machine attributes those later differences to the memory.

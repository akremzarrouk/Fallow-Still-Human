# Decision sensitivity: results

Generated by `SensitivityExperimentTests`. Every decision here is a decision a morning actually took, weighed again by the unchanged deliberator with the urgency of **one** want set to another value, everything else identical: the same mind in the state it decided in, the same percept, the same other wants, the same intention carried in, the same random stream. Urgency is swept over [0, 0.995], which is every value the motivator can produce (its curves approach 1 and never reach it), and probed at 1.5, 3 and 10, which it cannot, only to tell "needs more than the scale allows" from "never".

**Preferred** is the option first among those considered: no randomness is involved in it. **Chosen** is what was picked with the random stream the morning used, which differs from the preferred option only when the top options are within the ambiguity band (0.08) and the seed settles it.

### 1.2 What moving one want across its whole range does

Each pair is one want at one decision, moved from 0 to 0.995 with nothing else changed.

- **moves the act**: the preferred option is different somewhere in the range.
- **already its act**: it adds to what is preferred; raising it confirms the act and removing it changes nothing.
- **outweighed within the scale**: it adds to something that is never preferred at any urgency a want can have, but would be past it (1.5, 3 or 10).
- **outweighed at any urgency**: never preferred even at 10.
- **held off by an intention**: the person arrived with an intention that held throughout, and nothing this want adds to is among what they were deciding between.
- **no candidate**: nothing available here is served by this want at any urgency.
- **candidate only past reach**: something here would be served by it, but only at an urgency no want can have (a walk refused by foresight until then).
